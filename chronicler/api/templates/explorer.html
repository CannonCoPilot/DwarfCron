Population Data Model — Three Tiers
Tier 1: entity_populations (DF-native race counts per civilization)

Aggregate race-level population counts recorded by DF in the legends XML. These represent the game engine's own census of each civilization — not individual-level, but race-level totals. Includes all races associated with a civ (e.g., dwarf, elf, goblin citizens).

Tier 2: hf_entity_links (organizational membership — who belongs to what group)

Individual-level links between historical figures and entities (civilizations, religions, site governments). Each link has a link_type indicating the nature of the relationship: member, former member, enemy, prisoner, etc. This is the basis for "Citizens" counts (alive current members with civic affiliation) and the "Members" tab (all links, filterable).

Tier 3: hf_site_links (physical site presence — who is at what site)

Individual-level links between historical figures and specific sites. Each link has a link_type indicating the nature of the physical attachment: home structure, occupation, seat of power, lair, hangout. Combined with entity membership, this forms the basis for the "Denizens" tab on site pages (Citizens + Residents + Visitors). Orthogonal to entity membership — a figure can be a member of a civ without having a site link, and vice versa.

Entity membership and site presence are orthogonal dimensions. Most historical figures have entity links (organizational membership) but few have site links (physical site attachment). Site links are recorded only for figures with specific structural associations.
