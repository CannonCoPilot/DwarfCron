Import Legends Data

Export your world's legends from Dwarf Fortress (Legends mode), then run:

# From your DwarfCron directory:

chronicler ingest --legends /path/to/region-legends.xml

# Or point to the directory containing both files:

chronicler ingest --legends /path/to/legends-folder/

# The tool auto-detects *-legends.xml and *-legends_plus.xml

To export legends: In DF, retire/abandon your fortress, enter Legends mode, press x to export XML, then p for detailed XML.

Both files are needed for full data: *-legends.xml + *-legends_plus.xml
