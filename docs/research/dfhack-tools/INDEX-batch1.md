- [agitation-rebalance](agitation-rebalance.md) — it counts any newly arrived unit already carrying `flags4.agitated_wilderness_creature` as a surface attack and resets `plotinfo.outdoor_irritation` (:249-251), so our cavern irruption must arm groups only after they arrive. It also clamps world-level cavern irritation, and its cavern roll runs about once a year, not once a season (:300-303).
- [assign-attributes](assign-attributes.md) — `reqscript('assign-attributes').assign({STRENGTH=2}, unit)` sets tiered physical and mental attributes on any unit, wild animals included, and the change is permanent. It reads race medians from raw text and ignores castes; `caste_raw.attributes.phys_att_range[ATTR][3]` is the reliable median.
- [colonies](colonies.md) — colonies sit inside `df.vermin.get_vector()` with an `amount` of about 18826, so vermin sums must filter `flags.is_colony` (`alpha4_sec7_blocks.py:824` does not). Placed colonies have no population reference, so our population lever cannot reach them.
- [deathcause](deathcause.md) — a wild animal's killer is in `df.incident.find(unit.counters.death_id).criminal`; scanning `world.incidents.all` for `type == Death` gives a one-shot predator→prey kill ledger. The tool crashes on death types 52-54 and its doc example calls a local function.
- [deteriorate](deteriorate.md) — `corpses` deletes CORPSE and vermin REMAINS, and `food` deletes every EGG and fish wherever they are, after 100 cycles. At the control panel's "0.15 all" that is about 18,000 ticks, enough to erase kill evidence over long rig sessions. `now` works even while the tool is disabled.
- [fix/wildlife](fix-wildlife.md) — it only targets units with `flags2.roaming_wilderness_population_source` set and `leave_countdown <= 0`, so the animals we place and our resident groups (flag cleared) are safe from it. Its `--dry-run` still adds to `world.populations.all` (:149-151), so never run it, even with `-n`, on a fort whose populations we measure.
