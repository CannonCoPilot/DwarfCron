- [plant](plant.md): `create` refuses water deeper than 3 and RIVER/POOL bed materials, and it replaces the tiletype with Shrub/Sapling. It cannot plant on our beds and would erase their tile evidence, so aquarium seaweed stays render-only. The docs' `--dryrun` is really `--dry-run`.
- [probe](probe.md): `probe -c x,y,z` is a read-only, cursorless tile dump (tiletypes, flow and salt, per-tile region via region_offset, savagery and evilness, occupancy, feature index). Run once through `dfhack.run_command_silent`, it is an independent witness for our tile classifications.
- [region-pops](region-pops.md): it multiplies or adds to `local_population.quantity` only. It never touches `quantity_max`, `flags.extinct` or the master `world_population` (count_min/count_max). It merges all layers and types per token, also scales plant entries, and tests innumerable on quantity, not quantity_max.
- [regrass](regrass.md): grass is a per-block `block_square_event_grassst` amount[16][16], not a plant. regrass refuses water deeper than 3 (shrub_drown_level ≈ 4), so DF grass cannot exist on our 7/7 beds, and it never touches the Grass population entries.
- [reveal-hidden-units](reveal-hidden-units.md): it clears `flags1.hidden_in_ambush` on every active unit. That un-hides our SNEAK/AMBUSHPREDATOR hunters and removes their sneak speed term, so never run it mid-experiment. Reading the bit without writing it is a free ambush metric.
- [teleport](teleport.md): `Units::teleport` fails silently on off-map sources, forces the unit prone onto an occupied tile, and resets idle_area. Only since r2 does it keep a source tile's occupancy flag while another unit remains there; on r1.1 it always cleared it.
