The Seasonal Wildlife Plugin

What the tool can be built to do now that the game's wildlife machine has been measured rather than guessed at; how each capability the brief asked for maps onto a lever that has a run number behind it; what it does and does not touch among caravans, sieges, visitors and megabeasts; and the experiments that still stand between the measurements and the code.

Every capability on the list is buildable, and all but two rest on levers already measured. The two that do not — the predation switch and the aquatic layer — were the ones the tool had written off, and both turned out to be real this week.

The engine the tool needs is smaller than the one it has. DF keeps a per-pair relation table that decides who hunts whom, honours a written residency timer, reads pack size at the draw, opens the surface within 450 ticks of the last held animal, and draws sea life through a wet map edge exactly as it draws deer through a dry one. The tool's job is to write five numbers at the right moments and to say, in its own window, what it wrote and why. What follows is the design for that, in the order it should be built, with the tests each step must pass and the experiments that must run first.

3 · Architecture

One engine, three layers, one scheduler. The layer is a key on every entry, every config value and every group, never a filter.

Land · feat −1, cave −1

Surface animals, birds, vermin. Rotated today. Gate: surface flag category.

Water · lake and ocean tiles

The water tile's own surface entries — fish, sharks, hippos, otters. Same gate as land. Dormant if the map edge is dry. River and pool feature entries stay data-only.

Caverns · cave 0, 1, 2

Three layers, three entry sets, their own gate categories. Waves spawn unrevealed. Managed after the player has seen one, or on opt-in.

Deep · innumerable

Magma sea and below. Shown, locked, never written.

Two arrows, both two-way: the engine reads and writes DF; the window reads and writes the engine's config and reads its ledger. Nothing in the window touches DF directly, which is what makes the engine testable headless on the rig.

The arming sequence, which is the heart of it

-- when a PREY group is released and ecology is on (v5.6) 1 snapshot the layer's entries; zero every entry but the prey's matched predators 2 if groups.pack > 0: write cluster_number = {pack, pack} on those predators 3 release the prey's hold → predators land within ~450 ticks 4 on the predator wave's arrival: restore entries and raws 5 nudge: if the two groups are > N tiles apart for M ticks, move the predators to walkable tiles within R of the prey (policy b, §4) 6 relate: for every predator × prey pair with a slot, rel_map[p][q].ur = rel_map[q][p].ur = PREDATOR_OR_PREY never for a unit with civ_id ≠ −1, never for two BENIGN castes 7 record the pairs in site config; rewrite them after a load (cache is unsaved) 8 ledger: "piggyback WOMBAT → 7 DINGO (pack 7), nudged at +3,000, 14 pairs related"

4 · Decisions that are the product's, not the measurement's

Should the tool move animals?

Without a nudge, predator and prey on a 4×4 embark usually expire without meeting (E11). With one, they meet. Three policies: (a) teleport on arrival, deterministic and visibly artificial at the edge; (b) nudge only after the groups have stayed far apart, gentler; (c) never, and accept that most pairings are a tableau. Recommendation: (b), default N = 40 tiles for M = 3,000 ticks, exposed as "Nudge predators toward prey", off until E18 has measured how it looks.

Should the ecology switch default on?

No. It changes what the player sees in a way the vanilla game rarely does on its own. Default off; the biome presets may turn it on for savage biomes, where the vanilla experience already includes wildlife violence.

What does "predation" mean in the window?

Exactly what was measured: a predator beside prey, related in DF's table, will attack it. The food-web tab says so and names the two writes. It never says or implies that DF hunts unaided across the map.

When is a cavern layer managed?

When the player has seen it: any of its entries carries discovered, or a tile in it is revealed. Before that the layer is shown locked with the count of species waiting. Opt-in overrides.

What if the map has no water at its edge?

The water layer is marked dormant at load, with the reason in the window and the roster still visible. OCEAN taught this: a coastal fort can hold 28 shark entries and never draw one.

Vermin?

Out of the ecology switch entirely; rotated and curated as today.

5 · Work packages, in the order they should ship

Each ends with a deployable version and a rig manifest that passes with the save byte-identical afterwards. Numbering is the build order; it matters.

  1. v5.6

    Ecology switch

    groups ecology on|off, default off. Adds steps 5–8 of the arming sequence to the piggy-back. Slot guard for the 500-slot table; wildlife×wildlife pairs only; pairs persisted and rewritten after load.

    Gate: T4 manifest on CTRL — ecology on, one prey wave then piggy-back, ≥1 prey death within 5,000 ticks in 3 of 3; ecology off, 0 of 3 (E11c's no-lever arm is the control). Blocked by E16, E17, E22.

  2. v5.7

    Standing herds, turnover, stuck sweep

    groups hold <token> <days> raises the countdown on a resident group; groups dismiss <token> zeroes it and DF refunds. The stuck sweep nudges a resident at countdown 0 that has not left in 6,000 ticks toward the nearest edge, or releases it to DF's own path. fix/wildlife cannot see unflagged units; this is ours.

    Gate: T5 — a herd held a season, dismissed, refund exact, no stuck unit at year end. Blocked by E19.

  3. v5.8

    Water and cavern rotation

    The roster tick and groups job run per layer with per-layer quota and gaps. Wet-edge detection at load; dormant layers say why. Cavern layers managed on visibility or opt-in. Cavern raids (units with invasion_id ≥ 0) excluded from every count.

    Gate: T6 on LAKE and OCEAN2 (a year, per-layer checker), T7 on CTRL caverns. Blocked by E20, E21, E25.

  4. v5.9

    Arrival patterns and quotas

    Pattern library on the scheduler — steady, burst, trickle, dawn, follow — and "about 2 land + 2 water" as max_concurrent per layer. Follow scales predator probability by prey biomass on the map, the brief's rule, now worth having because the switch makes it matter.

    Gate: T8 — a season under each pattern; waves.tsv distributions differ as designed. Blocked by E24.

  5. v6.0

    The window

    Layer selector on every tab. Biome presets at first run (roster targets, pattern, ecology default, from the embark's biome family and savagery). Ecology tab with the switch, the nudge policy, pack size, and the live list of related pairs with their cache reading. Live-tab hotkeys for piggy-back, pack, hold, dismiss, next wave. Ledger tab. Undo ring for roster edits. Overlay links between predator and prey groups.

    Gate: the T5 user stories — a player does each thing end to end without the console.

  6. publish

    A remote and a submission

    The repo has never had a remote. Push it; write the DFHack script-pack submission; file the create-unit defect upstream while at it.
