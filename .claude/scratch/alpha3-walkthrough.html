    Window keys: what changed

    None. The GUI script’s HotkeyLabels are identical between v6.6 (f22fa30) and v7.0 (1e65e03); the window changed in four places only: the ODDS column, the Live tab’s groups line, the Fill hint, and the Add invasive status line. Three hotkey labels on the Roster and two on the species detail draw clipped (2.1, 3.1); they were clipped on v6.6 too and round 1 passed them.
