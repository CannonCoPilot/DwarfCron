New Rules Rebuild

Make the New Rules the one feeding test the tool uses: to grow each season's cast, to write who hunts whom in play, and to feed vermin. The Food Web Explorer becomes the specification, and the tool is generated from the same rules.

Why a rebuild, not another rule

Decisions for you

Six choices change what gets built. Each has my recommendation; nothing below starts until you have answered D1–D4.

D1What makes one animal hunt another, with the ladder gone

Today's hunting-pair rule already checks: the hunter is armed (a predator not wholly BENIGN, a raptor, a predator seat, a hunting cavern civ race), its habitat reaches the prey's, two water animals share a water body, a raptor takes nothing over twice its mass, civ races are spared, the hunting group weighs at least 5% of the prey, and every one of your bars and diets applies. Then it also requires the ladder.

Recommendation: keep every check above, drop the ladder, and add one size rule in its place: prey no heavier than the hunting group (group size × body mass) times a factor k. Before you choose k, the explorer shows each value's effect (links per predator, candidates linked, species whose cast rate moves) so the choice is made on numbers.

D2Mass and numbers: keep it in New Rules?

It was the coupling nudge's test (off by default) and is broad: alone it links 82% of animal candidates. Its vermin side rules duplicate the vermin rule's.

Recommendation: drop it from New Rules once D1's rule exists; keep its vermin side rules inside the vermin rule, where they already run.

D3How a cast grows on the web

Growth adds a candidate linked to any member (it eats one or is eaten by one); a slot nothing links to is filled anyway; caverns guarantee one predator and one prey.

Recommendation: keep that shape, and extend the cavern guarantee to every layer: each predator seated in a cast has at least one prey in that cast. Measure how often the unlinked fill is used under D1 before deciding whether it stays.

D4Evil apexes: matched by their raws, or added by wiring only?

The engine lets the grimeling, nightwing and harpy past the unliving filter so they match their raw biomes; the explorer adds them only where it wires them.

Recommendation: the engine's way (it is what runs in the game); the explorer follows.

D5What the rebuild removes

Recommendation: delete the tier ladder as a feeding test (ROSTER.FP.eats), the v7.1 roster links (ROSTER.linkable, gobble edges by guild), and DF's HUNTS_VERMIN writes (DF lets it take any vermin, which breaks your flying-vermin rule). The coupling nudge stays off, per the 16 Oct ruling. The FREQUENCY ladder sets arrival odds, not feeding, and stays as it is.

D6Where the one copy of the rules lives

Recommendation: a rules file in the seasonal-wildlife repo (rules/new-rules.json). A generator writes it into a Lua table that ships in the package (no file reading at run time, so nothing slow and nothing that can fail on load), and the desk and explorer read the same file. A rule change becomes one edit plus regeneration.

The rebuild, in order

Branch v8-newrules from c125091; release key 8.0.0. Each phase ends on a check that must pass before the next starts. As always, a phase is finished only when it is in the packaged zip, not on the dev copy.

  1. A

    Write the rules down once

    • Move every rule table into rules/new-rules.json: diets, bars, never-lists, role, guild and habitat overrides, extra and limited biomes, evil apexes, wetland ground, vermin types and sides, LINK_AS, the apex lists, and D1's size factor.
    • The desk and the explorer load it; their hand-kept copies are deleted.
    • Build D1's animal rule in the explorer, with a switch for k, and publish the measurements for your choice.

    ExitExplorer from the rules file, desk parity exact, and your choice of k (D1) recorded.

  2. B

    One feeding test in the engine

    • Generate RULES (Lua) from the file; write RULES.eats(a, b, layer) returning the reasons, as the explorer's does.
    • Route every caller through it: cast growth, the cast edge set, the hunting-pair filter, vermin selection. Delete the predicates D5 removes.
    • A conformance test: the explorer exports every (hunter, prey, layer) verdict on a set of forts; the engine harness computes its own and the two must agree on every pair.

    ExitConformance 100% on the test forts; fixes, controls, water, onoff and gui suites green; luacheck at or under 37.

  3. C

    What the tool writes in play

    • The hunting-pair write (ecology) relates only pairs RULES allows among the units present.
    • Vermin classes written from RULES' vermin verdicts; no HUNTS_VERMIN.
    • Verdicts cached per fort and cast, so the write stays a table lookup under FUSE's 250 ms warning.

    ExitA rig probe on a new embark: every written pair and every vermin class is one RULES allows; no pass over budget.

  4. D

    The cast builder on New Rules

    • Growth, seed and guarantees as D3 decides, using RULES.
    • With the same site and year seed, the engine and the explorer build the same 28 casts.

    ExitIdentical casts engine vs explorer on the test forts, every season and layer.

  5. E

    Validation in play

    • New embarks only (startdwarf 50, caverns breached at the start, embark size 4), v7.6.6 and v8 on the same tiles, 5 reps per arm, with a receipt that each fort's subjects are on the map.
    • Measured: casts built at embark match the explorer's; every predator kill is a pair RULES allows; no barred pair ever recorded; vermin eaten by allowed consumers; frame time per pass.

    ExitZero disallowed kills or relations across all reps; frame budget held. A rep with no data is marked pending, not passed.

  6. F

    The interface catches up

    • Rev 5's step 2: a Casts view (seven layers × four seasons) in the window and web page, now with each cast's links and why; every switch in the controls registry.

    ExitWindow and web harnesses extended and green; panel and page round trips on the rig.

  7. G

    Package and release

    • Validator claims for v8 (casts, rules conformance, writes), then validate-full at 0 FAIL.
    • Build the zip, install it as a player would (dev copy removed), and play one packaged year on a new fort with off at the end.
    • Docs (USAGE, STATE, HANDOFF, changelog); merge on your word.

    ExitThe packaged zip passes its year; this is the finished deliverable.

How it fits the beta plan (rev 5)

Rev 5's step 0 items 0.1–0.3 were built on 16–17 Oct; 0.4 (caps count groups) is open. Livestock as prey (step 1.2) and civ-race hunting (step 1.6) both depend on the feeding test, so the rebuild goes first and the open items resume on the rebuilt engine.

rev 5 step 0.1–0.3 (built)→rebuild A–E→step 0.4→step 1→F (= step 2)→G (= step 3)→beta play-through plan

Your 16 Oct rulings carry over: problems are solved one at a time by tuning what exists, all nudges stay off by default, irruptions and livestock as prey stay on, and the play-through plan is written last.
