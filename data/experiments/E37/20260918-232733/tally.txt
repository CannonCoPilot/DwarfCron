the noise floor — how far apart IDENTICAL replicates land
(same arm, same config, same fort: whatever separates these is the rig, not the tool)
  off_baseline: rep 1 250.7, rep 2 295.8  -> spread 45.1 fps (16.5%)
     at effectively the SAME load (53.5 vs 53.9 units, 0.8% apart) — this spread is pure rig noise
  on_baseline: rep 1 297.4, rep 2 268.8  -> spread 28.6 fps (10.1%)
     at effectively the SAME load (51.0 vs 49.9 units, 2.3% apart) — this spread is pure rig noise
  off_crowded: rep 1 192.3, rep 2 273.2  -> spread 80.9 fps (34.7%)
     loads differed by 14.4% too (85.2 vs 73.7 units) -> implies -7.05 fps/animal; NOT usable as a noise estimate
  on_crowded: rep 1 267.4, rep 2 254.5  -> spread 12.8 fps (4.9%)
     at effectively the SAME load (88.5 vs 88.2 units, 0.3% apart) — this spread is pure rig noise
  => RESOLUTION FLOOR: 16.5%, from the 3 pair(s) that ran at matched
     load. An on/off gap smaller than this is NOT a cost;
     it is the rig. Report it as 'below the noise floor', never as a measured cost.
