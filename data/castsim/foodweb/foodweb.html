How the cast builder decides
  1. Gates. A creature is a candidate when it is wild and natural, its BIOME (with the tool's own additions and limits) names one of the fort's biomes, and it passes that biome's savagery, good/evil and extinct rules.
  2. Layers. Land-ground, land-flying, ocean, freshwater (one cast: lake, else river or brook, else murky pool) and caverns 1 to 3 (your cavern picks, or DF's raw depth).
  3. Slots. Each layer has a slot table (apex, meso, grazer, ground prey, flier, fish, pelagic, shore, waterbird, swimmer, sentient, vermin); a creature's habitat, guild and apex eligibility say which slots it can take.
  4. Apex. A weighted draw among the eligible apexes in season: the square root of group size (body × mean pack) against the largest, times the pick weight.
  5. Seed. One high-value species (war-trainable, or the top share by pet value or by body size; never sentient), not on land-flying.
  6. Growth. Candidates linked to any member (it eats a member or a member eats it) fill the emptiest slot, by pick weight; a slot nothing links to is filled anyway. Pick weight: pack hunters ×pack bonus, animal people ×, giants ×, ×repeat weight per cast already holding the species this year, and in caverns the weirdness tilt.
  7. Cavern web guarantee. A cavern cast with no prey or no predator takes one.
  8. Vermin. What the members eat first, one per vermin class, then any.

Raws links are the only creature-to-creature diet tokens in DF 53.16's vanilla raws: GOBBLE_VERMIN_CLASS (kiwi, hedgehog, pangolin, duck, goose, peafowl and turkey eat the EDIBLE_GROUND_BUG vermin: ant, beetle, large roach, thrips), GOBBLE_VERMIN_CREATURE (unused in vanilla) and the peregrine's DIVE_HUNTS_VERMIN (any vermin). CARNIVORE, LARGE_PREDATOR and BONECARN name no prey; SPECIFIC_FOOD names plants. Engine ladder links are the v7.6.1 engine's feeding test, kept for comparison: a higher trophic tier eats a lower one when its habitat reaches the other's; shore animals and waterbirds take aquatic creatures up to their own size; vermin by diet class. Growth in the simulator follows the links you choose under Build casts. Not modelled: DF's own stocking on a real fort (stocked water entries, the zone read from stocked species), exhaustion stand-ins, placed thieves and apexes, and everything after the cast (FREQUENCY, caps, pauses). Source: castsim foodweb_export.py and foodweb_sim.js, checked against the desk simulator (exact pools, layers and slots; cast rates within sampling noise).
