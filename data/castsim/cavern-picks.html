Cavern Layer Picks

Every species the full pool can put in a cavern, with its weirdness score, the slots it can fill and what the rig cast and saw arrive. Mark the caverns you want each species to be able to appear in. Your picks are saved as you make them, and Claude reads them back to change the casts. A pick outside DF's raw depth range is allowed and flagged: DF stocks a species only inside that range, but on the rig it drew species the tool placed outside it. The proposed column is Claude's suggested change for a species, with the reason; use one, or all of them, and the change saves like any pick. A species marked not picked yet has no saved pick, so the tool still uses its raw depth for it: pick its caverns, or press keep raw depth to confirm them as they are.
