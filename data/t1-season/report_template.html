Seasonal Wildlife 7.2 · test item 1 · 7 October 2026

Smaksmo Oxdu Season Test

Four 16x16 forts in Smaksmo Oxdu (region19) and the 6x6 waterfall fort you embarked (Whitepaged), each run from mid-spring through the change into summer with every Seasonal Wildlife feature on at its defaults, reveal applied and 30 dwarves. An in-game recorder logged every creature that is not a citizen every 100 game ticks: who arrived, left, fought, killed and died, and where. The tool's own ledger and roster were dumped at the end of each run.

Summary

Every fort reached summer with all 30 dwarves alive. The river + ocean fort is its second attempt: the first lost every dwarf to cavern jabberers the tool teleported to the surface (finding 1). Cavern-surface teleports counts units that moved between a cavern and the surface more than 15 levels within 1,000 ticks, faster than any can walk.

Findings

  • Fixed

    The ecology nudge teleported cavern predators to the surface. With "livestock as prey" on (enable turns the opt-ins on), the nudge let the fort's own animals through its same-realm check. Cavern-2 jabberers and crundles were moved ~80 levels up beside them; the jabberers struck down all 30 dwarves in late spring on the first river + ocean fort. Now the nudge never moves a unit out of its realm (seasonal-wildlife e494d331). Cavern-surface teleports: 9 on that attempt, 0 on every fort since.

  • Fixed

    Waterfall streaks ran several ways at once, and the falling water looked like dry ground. Each lip tile pointed at its nearest top and the river's dashes crossed them; the falling water had no cell of its own, so through DF's translucent sprite it showed the gorge floor. Now one heading per edge, true diagonals, and a white-water curtain over the falling tiles (seasonal-wildlife 749384b). See Waterfall look.

  • Works

    The season change rotates the roster as designed. On summer 1 every fort applied the summer roster; 14-28 summer species per fort had no stock left in the world, and each handed its niche to an off-season species of the same layer and role (on the waterfall fort: troll → molemarian, hippo → tigerfish, jaguar → hyena). Seasons lent in spring (9-105 per fort) went back. Details under each fort's The season change.

  • Works

    The waterfall fort ran clean. 30 of 30 dwarves into summer, one cat lost, a median 86 fps (lowest 54), 108 arrivals (64 surface, 44 caverns), 30 deaths, 24 of them in the caverns; molemarians the top killers (13). No unit moved between a cavern and the surface.

  • Fixed

    Departures were unnamed in this morning's tables. When DF drops a unit from its unit list the recorder logs it as ?, because it is already gone. The analysis now names each one from its earlier record (all 1,403 resolved), drops removals of the already dead (4) and keeps DF's underworld demons out by race. This page uses the corrected numbers; the per-fort summaries are regenerated.

  • Open

    Frame rate on 16x16 forts. Median 18-29 fps with lows of 1-4, against 86 on the 6x6 fort. The tool's worst pass was 543 ms on the waterfall fort. For tuning.

  • Open

    Minks drown in the lake in a loop. The water layer draws minks into the lake at the map edge (5 levels deep); its balance and feed weights multiply the species x20 while the role is missing, each one drowns, and the role stays missing. 55 minks drowned on the lake fort. Ospreys drown too, 2-4 per fort. For test item 4.

  • Open

    The cavern gate cannot hold DF's waves. The cap is 5 groups per cavern; the ledger shows 23-31 groups in cavern 2 while the gate sends off one gated wave a pass. For test item 2.

  • Open

    The brook fort's water census lists no brook (23 rivers, 282 pools), so whether the brook rule (small freshwater animals only) saw it is unverified. For test item 4.

  • Note

    An 11-level river drop is not a waterfall. The river-drop fort (picked from region elevations) came down in steps: no surface mist, no falling column. The waterfall fort here is the one you embarked.
