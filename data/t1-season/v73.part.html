v7.3 season casts: first runs

v7.3 makes the cast the season's roster: per layer and season a small set grown from a high-value seed (your ruling of 7 October: the pool is not the roster), every other species resting. These runs check that arrivals follow the cast. Groups come from the tool's ledger: a tool draw (water) or a DF wave the tool found (land, caverns). Units come from the recorder and include births. A stand-in is a species the exhaustion check handed a cast member's niche.

Result. Arrivals follow the cast. On the two runs with the fixed engine (the waterfall fort into winter, the new brook fort into summer), every land and water arrival and all but one cavern arrival belonged to its season's cast. The exceptions were a troll 291 ticks after the change and DF's embark-time residents, which were sent off at once. At each change the tool sent off the groups whose species left the cast (10-13 a change). The first run found a bug: at autumn 1 the exhaustion check ran before the season's apply and handed 17 of the 22 autumn cast species' niches to out-of-cast stand-ins for the whole season. That is fixed (seasonal-wildlife 6e1ffb2), and the same bug explains the summer-1 hand-overs in test item 1 (corrected under Findings). Still open: 10 giant wrens on the waterfall fort in summer, a winter-only species, source pending.
