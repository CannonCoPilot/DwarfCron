Alpha Four Progress page
"Off" does not yet put everything back.

Disable, off and All off restore raws, flags and units, but not the population stock the tool holds at 0 for resting and out-of-season species: those entries stay empty and marked extinct in the saved world. The code says "every write put back". This is the first fix for a safe off switch.

M2 · roster P2 · rotation P9
Under casts, the ladder and the apex scheduler sit idle.

Casts write who may come and when, but not the FREQUENCY ladder or the apex keys; nothing calls roster build on its own. A v7.3 fort runs on raw FREQUENCY with no apex scheduling, so the pyramid targets the study set are not being steered.

M1 · roster P1
The cavern gate is a weight, not a gate.

Holding a full cavern cuts every species' FREQUENCY by the same factor, which keeps their ratios; DF keeps sending waves from sources the gate cannot see, and the trim removes one group a day. A bug makes the trim send off the new season's own cast after a turnover. 23-31 groups in a cavern capped at 5 is the result.

M2 · rotation P2-P4
Air-breathers are placed on the lake bed.

Every tool water draw enters at the deepest full-water tile, with no check that the species breathes water. Minks drown, the mix sees a missing predator and boosts minks up to ×20, and the loop repeats: 55 minks drowned on one lake fort.
