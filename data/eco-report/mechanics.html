M1Roster building and season casts

Three roster paths exist side by side: the old biome preset, the guild builder (roster build) and, since 7 October, the season casts. On a new fort enable builds casts, and they are regrown at each new year. Casts decide who may come and when; they do not set how often (the FREQUENCY ladder) or schedule apexes.

The pool

getEmbarkRegions gathers the site's region tiles and all nine neighbours of every block (DF draws from all of them); buildPool marks each species in the embark (DF stocked it) or merely eligible (the biome fits; used only by Add invasive). classify fills habitat, role, guild, mass, animal-person root, giant, civ, raw depth, lava-only and the 83 extinct corrections. Only the "natural" class is managed by default.

The season cast

  • Rule. The pool is not the roster. Each season the tool grows one cast per layer (land with flying, water, cavern); a cast species is active in the seasons it is cast in, and every other managed species rests.
  • Growing it. A weighted seed is drawn among high-value in-season species (pet value ≥ 200, war or hunting trainable, mass ≥ 1,000,000 cm³, or ivory, pearl, silk or shell). The cast grows through prey and predators, emptiest slot first, then 2-3 vermin a member eats. Weights: pack hunters ×3, last season's members ×0.25 (turnover), giants ×0.2, animal people ×0.1.
  • Size. At most 13 / 12 / 12 with vermin (land / water / cavern); the sentient slot never fills with the default settings, so 12 / 11 / 11 in practice. The desk's means were 8.8 + 2.7, 8.2 + 2.0 and 9.0 + 2.8.
  • Resting. Land and water species out of the cast are held at stock 0; cavern species drop to raw FREQUENCY 1 (or 0).
  • Storage. Casts are saved with the fort and reloaded (fixed 8 Oct, b0895b9). cast status, cast show SEASON, cast rebuild [salt], cast restfreq 0|1, cast on|off are console-only.

The guild builder (still there)

ROSTER.build fills guild slots per layer from a weighted seed, writes a FREQUENCY ladder in units (FREQUENCY ÷ mean group size) toward the pyramid targets, with a biomass guard and a floor of 1, and no apex step (R34): apexes are placed by the apex scheduler against a presence target. Under casts it runs only when you type roster build, and the casts then overwrite its roster at the next new year while its odds stay.

What it writes, and what goes back

Raw FREQUENCY (cavern rest, ladder), cluster sizes, extinct corrections, skill caste tokens, NO_<season> flags (cleared under seasons_own), and population entry quantities and extinct flags. Disable and All off restore the raws and flags; they do not restore population quantities, so a fort switched off keeps its resting species empty in the saved world.

Most consequential findings. The ladder and apex scheduler are idle under casts (P1). cast off comes back on after a reload because of an and/or idiom on a boolean (P3). Hand edits, stand-ins and newly enabled layers get no predator-prey writes, because the ecology keeps only cast edges (P12, P13). The raw NO_<season> filter can silently switch off after an undo or an extinct toggle (P9).

1. The pool: which species an embark can hold

1.1 The region tiles: getEmbarkRegions (:1311-1391)

The function returns regionSet ("rx:ry" → biome id) and biomeSet (biome id → true). It is cached per site and map size in CACHE.region. It collects:

  1. The site record's tiles. The site's global bounds divided by 16, from df.world_site.find(site_id) (:1316-1325).
  2. Every block's region tiles, all nine neighbours. For each map block, all nine region_offset[0..8] values, giving region_pos + (off % 3 - 1, off // 3 - 1) (:1335-1343). The note at :1329-1334 records why all nine: X1 found cavies drawn from a neighbour tile that a one-offset read had missed.
  3. For cave entries only, a wider set. The full 3×3 neighbourhood of every site tile (CACHE.caveNum, :1355-1366; from Alpha Four 3.8, where trolls and devourers came from 168:173 and 170:173). Surface entries keep the block set.

regionSet is also kept in numeric form (CACHE.regionNum, :1348-1350), so the membership test per entry does not allocate.

1.2 Population entries and layers
  • managedPop(pop, regionSet, layers) (:1428-1438) decides which regional entries the tool manages. An entry is managed when all four hold:
    • its type is Animal, Vermin, VerminInnumerable or ColonyInsect (POP_TYPES, :1424);
    • its region tile is in the set: the cave set for cave_id >= 0, the block set otherwise;
    • quantity_max < INNUMERABLE (10,000,001, :526): uncountable clouds are never touched;
    • its layer is in the asked-for layer set.
  • layerOf(pop) (:1403-1409):
    • cave_id ~= -1 → cavern if the cave's layer_depth <= 2, else deep (the magma sea and the underworld);
    • feature_idx ~= -1 → water;
    • otherwise land.

Ocean life and many river fish carry no feature index. The pool therefore files them under their land key; v7.3's turnover had to learn this (8817eb4, :12788-12791).

  • CACHE.managedPops() (:2959-2973) indexes every managed entry of every layer once per map, as {i, race, key, layer}. It is rebuilt when populations.all grows (Add invasive). Every season pass walks this index.

1.5 Ecology class and locking
  • V7.ecologyClassRaw (:1588-1615) sorts each raw into one class, testing in this order:
    • generated (GENERATED);
    • mega (MEGABEAST, SEMIMEGABEAST, TITAN);
    • night (the NIGHT_CREATURE_* flags);
    • unliving (NOT_LIVING, or NO_EAT + NO_DRINK without GRAZER or CARNIVORE);
    • mythic (FANCIFUL, GOOD, EVIL);
    • unclassified (no body size, or no BIOME);
    • natural (everything else).
  • ecoOf (:1678-1688): a player override can only bring a creature in as natural. A mythic creature becomes natural through V7.alignedNatural (:1657) in three cases:
    • FANCIFUL alone, when v7 fanciful is on;
    • a cave-only GOOD or EVIL species, when cave_aligned is on;
    • surface GOOD or EVIL wildlife on a region of its own alignment, when aligned is on. V7.alignment (:1620) reads each region tile's evilness: < 33 is good, ≥ 66 is evil; the world_region's own flags count too.
  • e.locked = cfg.classes[eco] ~= true (:2360). The defaults (:818) manage only natural.
    • A locked species is never allowed (isAllowed :2417).
    • V7.sanitizeLocked (:1742) drops a saved allow or assign for one at load.
  • defaultAllow(e) = in the embark, not HAS_ANY_MEGABEAST or night, and not locked (:2416).

2.2 How a species gets its slot: ROSTER.slotOf(cfg, e, layer) (:7685-7705)

The rules are tested in this order:

  1. Cavern civ race with civ_prey on: AL or AW becomes ML, or MW when aquatic. A civ race never holds an apex slot.
  2. sn_slots on, sentient and not a monster: SN (tier ≥ 2) or SNP.
  3. Water part:
    • the curated aquatic_apex kind pelagic → APE;
    • apex or fisher → APX;
    • any other AW, AL or ML → MW (R49 demotes the sea lamprey);
    • otherwise the guild.
  4. Flying part: APX if ROSTER.flyApex (:7679) holds: an RP that is not sentient, not a cave species, not a scavenger (vulture, buzzard, kea, raven), with mass ≥ fly_apex_min (2000) or boosted. Otherwise the guild.
  5. Land and cavern parts: AL, AW or boosted → APX; otherwise the guild.

Tiers (ROSTER.TIER :7667): APX, APE, AL and AW are 3; ML, MW, RP and SN are 2; every other guild is 1; V is 0. Trophic level (ROSTER.LEVEL :7668): apex slots are A, ML/MW/RP/SN are C, everything else is H.

2.3 Which pairs count as a food link: ROSTER.linkable(e, p, cfg, layer) (:7794)

Three tests must all pass:

  • e's tier is strictly above p's;
  • MODEL.reaches(e, p) holds (:2565): neither is mega or night, p is not a sponge, the same ecology class, the same realm (surface or cavern), the habitat REACH table (:2531) allows it, and shared water when both are aquatic;
  • in the caverns, the two share a cavern depth.

There is no size test. Size enters only through preference() in the pick weight.

2.4 Seed and pick weight

The weight. ROSTER.pickWeight(e, roster) (:7829-7841) is:

w = (pack ? pack_bonus : 1) × (AP not civ ? ap_pick : giant ? giant_pick : 1) × (1 + Σ preference over linkable pairs with the roster so far)

  • The pack bonus (ROSTER.packBonus :7789): tier ≥ 2, not BENIGN, and raw cmax > 1 (R41).
  • preference(e, p) (:2576): exp(−ln(M_prey / (0.4 M_pred))² / (2 × 1.2²)). Vermin score 0.05.

ROSTER.weightedPick (:7842) is a weighted random draw (rng:drandom).

The seed (:8107-8114): the first slot in table order that has candidates with any link in the layer's candidate set. The seed is a weighted pick among those candidates, against an empty roster.

2.5 Fill passes, the fallback when filling stalls, and unfilled slots
  1. Minimum pass (fillPass(false) :8116-8130). Each slot fills to its min by weighted picks from the candidates not yet used.
  2. When filling stalls (:8133-8158). If a slot is still below its min and the last pass made no progress, the builder takes the first unused candidate of that slot that links to any unused candidate p. It seats the candidate, and seats p too if p's slot has room (pairsSeeded). The loop has a 200-iteration guard.
  3. Maximum pass (while fillPass(true) :8159). Every slot fills to its max.
  4. Unfilled slots (:8161-8172). Each slot still below its min is reported with one of three reasons: `no candidate in pool, no edge in pool, or pool exhausted or no partner seated`. The builder then carries on: an unfilled slot is never fatal.
2.6 Link guard and the vegetation link (:8175-8208)

The builder repeats this step until nothing changes, with a guard of 200 rounds. A seated species with no link to any other seated species goes through three checks:

  • If it is a herbivore (level H, not CARNIVORE) and V7.vegSupports(e) holds, it stays, "kept by the vegetation link".
  • Otherwise it is swapped for an unused candidate of the same slot that does link.
  • Failing that, it is dropped.

The vegetation index is V7.vegSurvey (:7438-7505): (region vegetation mean + grass share %) / 2.

  • The region vegetation is read through getRegionBiome.
  • The grass share is the grass floor tiles over the outdoor walkable floor of the surface band.
  • Shrub and tree counts are reported but not used in the index.
  • The survey has a 1000 ms deadline and is cached per map.

The need is V7.vegSupports (:7511): need = clamp(20 + 15 × log10(mass / 100,000 cm³), 10, 90), and the species is supported when index ≥ need.

2.7 Outgun check (:8211-8224)

ROSTER.outgunPairs (:7805) finds every seated predator-prey pair where the prey is not BENIGN and the prey's mass × group is at least v7.outgun (2.0) × the predator's mass × group. Each such pair is logged as a build ledger line.

With v7 outgun_cap on (it is off by default), the builder also sets cfg.group_size[prey] to floor(predMass × factor / preyUnitMass). PATTERN.sizeApply then writes that as the raw CLUSTER_NUMBER on the next groups pass.

2.8 The FREQUENCY ladder: ROSTER.ladder(cfg, layer, members, ctx) (:7882-7971)

The builder turns the seated species into FREQUENCY values (1-100) for the prey (H) and meso-predator (C) levels. Apexes are handled separately, at step 8.

  1. Group by slot. Apex-level members (APX, APE, AL, AW) go to a separate list.
  2. Base weight for each H/C member.
    • b = LADDER22[part][slot] (:7874-7879). For SN or SNP members the code looks up their guild instead; a slot code with no entry falls back to 5.
    • On water, a PE member's b is tripled when ctx.deep (:7900).
  3. Mass scaling within its slot. scale = clamp((mass / geometric-mean mass of its slot)^−0.75, 0.25, 4) (:7901).
  4. Animal-people and giant multiplier. ×ap_freq or ×giant_freq, applied inside the level (:7906).
  5. Split between levels. The pyramid's H:C shares are normalised, from roster.pyramid[pk], where pk is ocean/lake on water (by ctx.ocean) and otherwise the part's own name.
    • If a level is empty, the other level takes 100%.
  6. Biomass guard (land and cavern only, :7918-7925). If uC × meanMass_C > guard × uH × meanMass_H (guard = 0.5), then the code sets r = guard × mH / mC, uC = r / (1 + r) and uH = 1 − uC, and reports binding = biomass guard.
  7. Unit share, then scaling to 0-100.
    • Each member gets x = levelShare × v / ΣlevelQ.
    • With roster.units on, x is divided by ROSTER.groupMean (:7862): the player's group size, else the raw cluster midpoint, not floored.
    • The result is rescaled so the largest x becomes 100, then rounded, and floored at 1 (:7935-7939).
  8. Apex members (R34: no step on the ladder).
    • apex_odds > 0 → that value is written.
    • Else a raw FREQUENCY above apex_raw_cap (5) is written as the cap.
    • Else nothing is written, and the raw's own FREQUENCY stands. The "raw" here is the snapshot's original if one is held (CAVERN.saved). An extinct apex reads its corrected value (:7944-7950).
  9. Report. info holds the targets, the binding constraint, the floored tokens, and the predicted carnivore share in waves and in units.

2.9 What the build writes (:8226-8251)

For every candidate in the part whose slot is in the table but was not chosen:

  • ROSTER.setActive(..., false), if it was active;
  • cfg.odds[key] = nil.

For every member:

  • ROSTER.setActive(..., true). A member with no season gets one default season (ROSTER.defaultSeason :2675);
  • cfg.odds[key] = ladder[key];

  • ROSTER.gobbleEdges builds the vermin edge table for the part;
  • one build ledger line is written;
  • the verb pushes one undo step (:18470), saves, and runs applyLive when rotation is on.

2.10 Apex scheduler (R9, R34): ROSTER.placeTick (:8893) and ROSTER.apex.decide (:8845)
  • When it runs. Once per game day from tick() (:4148-4152), for each part with apex_keys, then for the R36 invasives (:8917-8923).
  • Presence. It measures the share of time an apex group of those races is on the map, counted by population source (ROSTER.apex.groupsOnMap :8702). DF's own waves count.
  • When it stops. Reached the cap (cap_calm 1 or cap_savage 2, chosen at :8905), or at target after 7 days of observation.
  • Otherwise. It places with probability 1 − exp(−λ·dt), where λ = P / ((1 − P) × residence), P = presence % / 100 (capped at 0.95), and residence is 21,500 ticks (:8862-8867).
  • What it places. A uniform random choice among the keys that are active, in season and stocked. The group is placed by ROSTER.placeGroup (:8749), which debits stock, enters land groups at the map edge and water groups by the water-body rule, and leads the group at once.
  • Presence targets: land 25%, flying 0%, water 20%, cavern 70%.
2.11 What the builder does not do
  • It does not run on enable, on load or on any timer.
  • It does not set seasons beyond the one default.
  • It does not touch other parts.
  • Its realm filter, ladder and apex keys are not used by the casts.

3.1 The rule

The header at :8259-8277 sets out the rule. The pool (the embark's species) is not the roster.

  • Each season, the tool grows a cast for each of the three roster layers (land, water, cavern).

  • Every other species those layers manage rests: inactive, with no season.

Non-vermin.

3.4 Who eats whom, and who can never be cast

ROSTER.castEats(a, b) (:8416-8429).

  • A vermin eats nothing.
  • If b is vermin, a eats it when ROSTER.vdiet(a.e, a.code, a.part) covers b's class. ROSTER.VDIET (:7980):
    • ML eats VG, VC and VF; MW eats VF; an aquatic APX eats VF; RP eats VB and VI;
    • a cavern LB eats VB, VI and VG;
    • vermin-root animal people eat VG, VI and VF;
    • DF's own gobblers eat VG and VC.
  • Otherwise a eats b under the builder's link rule: a higher tier, MODEL.reaches, and a shared cavern depth. Each species takes its tier from its own part, so a raptor (flying part) can take ground prey (land part).

ROSTER.castExcluded (:8431). A species is never castable this season if:

  • it has no cast slot; or
  • none of its food-web neighbours is castable. This check is repeated until stable.
3.5 Growing one cast: ROSTER.castGrow(cfg, pool, layer, season, prev, r) (:8451-8566)
  1. The food web. Build eats and the undirected neighbour map nb over every pair of candidates.
  2. The weight of a candidate (:8472-8479): 1 × cast.pack (3) if packBonus holds × cast.carry (0.25) if it was in the previous season's cast × cast.giant (0.2) if a giant × roster.ap_pick (0.1) if an animal person that is not civ. Unlike the builder, there is no preference sum over links.
  3. The seed (:8495-8507). A weighted pick among non-vermin, non-excluded candidates for which ROSTER.highValue holds; if there are none, a weighted pick among all of them. ROSTER.highValue (:8349-8371) counts a species as high-value for any of:
    • pet value ≥ cast.hv_petvalue (200). For a giant, the root animal's pet value is read (DF gives every giant 500);
    • training: TRAINABLE_WAR or TRAINABLE_HUNTING;
    • parts: mass ≥ cast.hv_mass (1,000,000 cm³), or a creature material named IVORY, PEARL, SILK or SHELL.

The answer is memoized in CACHE.hv by token.

  1. Growth (:8509-8530), with a guard of 400 rounds:
    • Pick a random member that has not been set aside.
    • Its candidates are its neighbours that are not vermin, not already cast, not excluded, and whose slot still has room.
    • If it has none, set it aside.
    • Otherwise take the candidates whose slot has the largest fractional room (the emptiest slot first). Among those, make a weighted pick, and log FROM -> TO (slot, its prey | its predator).
    • Stop when every slot is full, or every member is set aside.
  2. Unfilled slots (:8531-8538): "have of want: none in season in the pool" or `"no link to the cast from the N in the pool"`.
  3. Vermin (:8540-8561). Take the vermin that cast members eat, shuffled with r:
    • first, one per distinct class;
    • then by how many members eat them;
    • up to L.vermin in all.
  4. Edges (:8562-8565): every cast-to-cast eats pair, kept as {PRED, PREY} tokens.
3.6 A year of casts: ROSTER.castYear(cfg, pool, year, why) (:8589-8633)

Growing the casts. For each layer that cfg.layers has on (land, water, cavern; deep never), for s = 0..3:

  • The random seed is fixed by seed = (site_id × 7919 + year × 104729 + s × 131 + #layer × 17 + cast.salt) % 2147483647 (:8602), so the same fort, year and salt always grow the same casts.
  • Carry-over: prev for spring is the stored winter cast for that layer (:8599-8600). Each later season uses the season before it.

Writing the roster. For every pool entry that a cast layer manages (ROSTER.castManages :8576 — its parts and vermin, and also locked or unpartitioned species of that layer, so that BLOOD_MAN and ELEMENTMAN_MUD rest instead of leaking in at their raw odds):

6. Problems, gaps and suspicious logic (with lines)

P1. Under season casts, the FREQUENCY ladder and the apex scheduler are idle unless someone runs roster build.

  • castYear writes only allow and assign (:8617-8625). It never writes cfg.odds or roster.apex_keys.
  • ROSTER.placeTick returns at once with no apex keys and no invasives (:8896-8899).
  • ROSTER.build has exactly one caller, the verb (:18429). On a v7.3 fort, every species therefore runs at its raw FREQUENCY, and no apex is scheduled.
  • If roster build is run, its cfg.odds and apex_keys survive every later castYear. The apex scheduler then places only keys the cast wants this season (:8871). A cast apex that the build did not pick is never scheduled.
  • The registry's own help says this: "It acts only after roster build seats apexes" (controls :155).

P2. Stock writes are not undone by off, disable or all off.

  • disableSched (:15950-15981) and PANEL.restoreAll (:16042-16059) restore FREQUENCY, cluster, flags and raws, but never population quantities.
  • Every resting or out-of-season entry stays at quantity = 0, extinct = true. Its value is held in the RESERVE until the tool runs again.
  • Yet turn_off prints "every job stopped and every write put back" (:16275), and the PANEL comment says "puts back everything the tool wrote" (:15994).

P3. cast off does not survive a save and load.

  • castSanitize uses c.cast[k] = (type(r) == type(v)) and r or v (:8322). With a saved enabled = false, this gives true and false or true, which is true.
  • So after a reload the casts are on again, and they rebuild at the next new year.
  • The same trap would hit any future boolean in CAST_DEF that defaults to true.

P4. cast off does not bring back "the preset roster" as its help says (:18340).

  • It only clears cast.enabled (:18347-18350). allow and assign stay as the casts left them: this year's casts become a fixed, all-year roster, and resting species stay inactive.
  • cast on with no casts prints a hint, but castOn stays false until cast rebuild.

P5. restfreq 0 reaches past "resting cavern species".

M2Seasonal rotation and arrival control

The daily job arms a timer for the exact season-change tick; the change gives back borrowed seasons, regrows casts at a new year, zeroes the out-of-season entries into the reserve, refills the in-season ones, and resets cavern frequencies. Between changes the groups pass (every 300 ticks) re-pins the holds, releases DF's gated waves on an adaptive clock, trims caverns and sends off off-cast groups once per season.

The jobs

  • Groups pass, every 300 ticks: re-pin holds, discovery and adoption, the release clock, the cavern gate and trim, the cast turnover, the exhaustion check.
  • Daily: the season timer, cavern FREQUENCY, the spill (off under casts), the apex scheduler, a registry rescan.
  • Ecology, every 3,000 ticks: the relation writes (M3). Scavenging every 200, curious thieves every 50, quiet fights every 10.
  • All are DFHack repeat timers behind fuses; the C++ plugin only bridges autostart and map load and schedules none of this.

One season change, in tick order

  1. The timer fires at the boundary (retrying 10-120 ticks until DF's own season turns).
  2. V7.ST.turn: spill borrows given back; at Winter → Spring the year's casts regrow; applyLive zeroes out-of-season entries into the reserve (extinct set) and refills in-season ones from it; CACHE.appliedStamp is set.
  3. CAVERN.apply: resting cavern species to restfreq, vermin always 1.
  4. Next groups pass: castTurnover zeroes the leave countdown of every tracked group whose species left the cast (water groups via their land key); DF walks them off. Nothing is teleported.
  5. The exhaustion check, now waiting for the apply's stamp, hands an empty cast niche to a stand-in.

The caps

Land and each water body type: floor(√embark tiles) + 1 groups, or one fixed cap. Caverns: 5 each. DF decides when a wave comes; the tool can only withhold the release of a gated wave (land, caverns) or draw a group itself (water). That asymmetry is why land overshoots slightly and caverns a lot.

Most consequential findings. The cavern trim counts groups already sent off at the turnover and so trims the new cast for days (P3, a bug with a one-line fix). The turnover runs once per season, so an off-cast group arriving after it stays all season (P4). The reserve is discarded when a DF refund lands just before the apply (P6). An error in the season turn stops rotation for the season, because the daily job calls it without a guard (P8). Disable leaves the world held (P9). enable turns on the deep layer against R60 (P15). Air-breathing "semiaquatic" species are placed on the deepest water tile and drown, and the mix then boosts them (P5).

0. The model in one page

Two drivers. Every layer gets its animals through one of two drivers.

  • Driver A: DF draws. This covers land, sea life listed on land entries, and caverns 1-3. DF sends a "wave" from a population source whenever no flagged animal of that source is on the map. A source is a region tile x feature x cave x population entry; see srcKey at sw:4307 and the gate notes at sw:4167-4187 and sw:12567-12575. The tool steers these draws three ways:
    • Who comes:
      • on the surface, by the regional entry's quantity and flags.extinct (applyLive sw:3027, holdOutOfSeason sw:3072);
      • in the caverns, by the creature raw's frequency (CAVERN.apply sw:3698). Entry quantity gates nothing underground (sw:3548-3557, sw:3614-3621).
    • When: by holding DF's own gate shut. A wave's animals keep the roaming_wilderness_population_source flag, and the tool clears that flag (a "release") only on its own clock (releaseGroup sw:4411, detachOldest sw:4424).
    • How many at once: by a per-layer group cap (QUOTA.groupsFor sw:3526). In the caverns there is also a frequency hold and a trim (V7.GRP.cavernPass sw:12591).
  • Driver B: the tool draws. This covers water bodies (ocean, lake, river or brook, pool), because DF never waves a feature entry in play (sw:4583-4586). The tool picks a species (ENGINE.candidates sw:5972, V7.WAT.mix sw:6394). It creates the animals with dfhack.units.create (PLACE.one sw:4821), debits the entry one per animal (sw:6125), and tracks them as a group under a per-body-type cap and clock (V7.waterTick sw:9280).

The roster is the season. Every managed key has an explicit cfg.allow[key] (true = active, false = inactive) and, when active, cfg.assign[key] = a list of seasons 0-3. ROSTER.wants(cfg, key, season) is the single test every pass uses: allow == true and the season is in assign (sw:2726). Keys:

  • land species are keyed TOKEN;
  • other layers are keyed water:TOKEN, cavern:TOKEN or deep:TOKEN (keyFor sw:1411).

The layer comes from the entry: cave_id gives cavern (depth 0-2) or deep (3+), feature_idx gives water, anything else is land (layerOf sw:1403).

v7.3 season casts. When cfg.cast.enabled and cfg.casts exist (ROSTER.castOn sw:8305), the roster is generated once a year:

  • For each layer (land, water, cavern) and each season, a "cast" is grown from a high-value seed through its prey and predators into fixed guild slots (ROSTER.castGrow sw:8451, slots sw:8282-8297).
  • A species in some season's cast is active with exactly those seasons.
  • Every other species the cast layer manages "rests" (allow = false, no seasons).
  • Locked species that would rest go into cfg.casts.rest (ROSTER.castYear sw:8589-8631; ROSTER.managedKey sw:2723).

2. The season deal

2.1 Who gets which season
  • Without casts (the preset, matrix or manual roster), seasons come from ROSTER.deal (sw:2665-2673). Species are sorted by climate lean within their group (realm|habitat|role, sw:2638-2640) and dealt one season each by quarter (ROSTER.quarterOf sw:2647, order Winter, Autumn, Spring, Summer = ROSTER.ORDER {3,2,0,1} sw:2631).
  • ROSTER.normalize (sw:2699-2714) makes the roster whole: every in-embark, unlocked species gets an explicit allow; an active one with no season gets one by ROSTER.defaultSeason (sw:2675); an inactive one is cleared of seasons. It runs at every enableSched, so at every load (sw:15933).
  • With casts, ROSTER.castYear (sw:8589) writes allow and assign for every pool entry a cast layer manages (ROSTER.castManages sw:8576-8586). Casts are reseeded per site, year, season and layer:
    • seed = (site*7919 + year*104729 + s*131 + #layer*17 + salt) % 2^31-1 (sw:8602);
    • a species in last winter's cast is weighted carry 0.25 (sw:8475), so the casts turn over year to year.
2.2 DF's NO_SPRING / NO_SUMMER / NO_AUTUMN / NO_WINTER versus seasons_own
  • DF honours NO_<season> at its own pick (ECO T2, cited at sw:2650-2653). classify records them per creature as noSeason[0..3] (sw:1822-1823). Classification is memoized per world in CACHE.class (sw:1796).
  • v7.seasons_own defaults to on (sw:840), and V7.grpSanitize forces it on at migration (sw:728). With it on:
    • ROSTER.fitSeason returns the dealt season unchanged (sw:2657-2663);
    • V7.apply clears every NO_<season> caste flag on every in-embark, unlocked species in the pool, recorded through V7.rec (sw:13833-13835);
    • the flags come back on disable, All off and world unload (V7.restore sw:13741, sw:15966, sw:16051, sw:17102);
    • Driver B ignores noSeason too (sw:5995-5999).
  • With it off, the deal moves a forbidden season to the next allowed one (sw:2661) and Driver B skips the species in that season.
  • Casts honour the raws whatever seasons_own says. ROSTER.castPool drops a species with noSeason[season] (sw:8393, sw:8406), and ROSTER.castReplaceOk refuses one as a stand-in (sw:8650). The flags are read from the memoized classification, so this holds only if CACHE.class was built before V7.apply cleared them (section 9, item P10).
2.3 What a season change does (V7.ST.turn, sw:4093-4109)

Called once per change by whichever comes first: the timer (V7.ST.now), the groups-pass fallback, or the daily job.

  1. lastSeason = s (first, before any work).
  2. Copy-on-write of the shared config (sw:4095).
  3. ROSTER.exhaustRoll: borrowed seasons given back, exhausted marks forgotten (sw:3106-3120).
  4. V7.WAT.spillRoll: water spill borrows given back (sw:7342-7358).
  5. If casts are on and casts.year ~= cur_year: ROSTER.castYear grows this year's casts and saves (sw:4098-4101). In practice this happens at Winter → Spring, or at the first apply after a load into a later year.
  6. applyLive(cfg, s) (sw:3027-3063):
    • For every managed entry on an enabled layer:
      • In season (ROSTER.wants):
        • a player stock (cfg.stock[key]) is written exactly, spread over the entries (RESERVE.set sw:3015), unless the key is exhausted;
        • an entry at quantity ≤ 0 is refilled from the reserve res[i], else the worldgen snapshot default_snapshot[key], else qtyFor = in_season_qty × weight / 50 = 100 by default (sw:2453, sw:3042-3048);
        • an entry above 0 is left as DF keeps it;
        • the reserve slot is then cleared and extinct = (quantity <= 0) (sw:3049-3050).
      • Out of season, inactive or resting: the quantity is saved into the reserve and the entry is set to quantity = 0, extinct = true, "both barriers" (sw:3053-3054).
    • It sets **CACHE.appliedStamp = year*4 + season** when season == cur_season (sw:3061).

4. Exhaustion and stand-ins

ROSTER.exhaustCheck (sw:3121-3193) runs every groups pass, after the apply stamp check.

  1. exhaustRoll (same stamp: no-op).
  2. For every key wanted this season (and not stock 0, not already done), sum the entries' live quantity. Every key at 0 is exhausted: x.done[key] = true.
  3. For each exhausted non-vermin key, it picks a stand-in:
    • Without casts: the same ROSTER.group (realm|habitat|role). The score is the log-mass distance, plus 100 when the guild differs.
    • With casts: ROSTER.castReplaceOk (sw:8635-8662). The stand-in must fill the same cast slot on the same layer and eat, or be eaten by, a member of this season's cast. A resting species may be chosen (cfg.allow == false is accepted under casts, sw:3155).
    • In both cases the stand-in must:
      • not be in season already;
      • not be done or promoted;
      • have live + reserve + snapshot stock > 0;
      • without seasons_own, not be forbidden that season by the raws.
  4. The stand-in gets the season added to its assign and allow = true. Its entries are restocked from reserve, snapshot or qtyFor, and extinct is cleared. It is recorded in x.promoted[key] = { season, for_key, resting } with an edit ledger line (sw:3167-3185).
  5. While x.done holds a key, applyLive does not refill it (ROSTER.exhaustHolds sw:3101-3104, sw:3041-3042).

Retirement (ROSTER.exhaustRoll sw:3106-3120, at the season change or at the first check of a new stamp):

  • The borrowed season is removed from each stand-in's assign, but never when that would leave an active species seasonless.
  • A stand-in that was resting goes back to rest (allow = false, assign nil, sw:3114).
  • done and promoted are cleared.
  • The stand-in's entries are then zeroed by the turn's applyLive, because it is no longer in season.
  • Its groups already on the map:
    • Under casts, the next castTurnover dismisses them (no longer wanted).
    • Without casts they stay until their own leave countdowns run out.

Caverns: a cavern stand-in's stock is opened at once, but its FREQUENCY stays held until the next daily CAVERN.apply, up to one game day later (exhaustCheck does not call CAVERN.apply).

6.2 The release clock (R31, V7.GRP.ctl/gapFor/dueTick/releaseGap sw:12344-12421)

Each layer key keeps its own controller. Its state lives in g.ctl[lk] in the groups record.

  • W, the mean stay, is an exponential mean (w_alpha 0.2) of finished groups' stays. Priors: land 34,500, water 27,000, cavern 30,000. Natives, seeded, trimmed and irruption groups do not feed it (sw:12372).
  • L* is redrawn uniformly in [cap - target_band, cap] (band 1) at every release, with a jitter 1 ± gap_jitter (0.35).
  • gap = W / L* × f × jitter, where:
    • d = L* - n (n = groups there now);
    • f = 1/(1 + catchup×d) when d > 0, else 1 + catchup×|d|;
    • the result is clamped to [gap_floor_days, gap_max_days] = [0.5, 20] days.
  • Each pass recomputes the due tick from the last release with the current n. The layer's timer only ever moves earlier. At n ≥ cap the clock returns nil, which pauses it.
  • adaptive off, or the burst pattern, uses PATTERN.nextGap: 5-20 jitter days (gap_min_days, gap_max_days), or burst gaps (sw:10236-10254).

Land release (sw:13268-13288). All of these must hold:

  • no coupling or pattern table restored this pass;
  • nLand < cap, where nLand = ENGINE.count(g,'land'), every land group except seeded (sw:6147-6151), so natives, placed apex groups and dismissed groups that have not left all count;
  • now ≥ g.next_wave_tick;
  • under the land ceiling.

Then detachOldest releases the oldest gated land group (sw:4424-4434), and its source can draw again.

gate_drain (v7.gate_drain, on): V7.drainGate (sw:4445-4464) then releases the next-oldest gated groups of the layer until at most one flagged animal remains. It releases at most room = cap - n - 1 extras (sw:13282).

6.3 The cavern gate (R44, V7.GRP.cavernPass sw:12591-12635)

Per depth d, with cap = cavern_cap (5):

  • Count: every cavern group of depth d except running irruption waves: DF waves (gated and released), natives, placed and adopted groups, and dismissed or trimmed groups not yet gone (sw:12595-12601).
  • Release: if a gated group exists and n < cap and the depth's clock is due, detachOldest(g,'cavern',d). The gap is multiplied by 0.5 while irruption pressure for that cavern is high (IRRUPT.gapDays sw:10938-10953, sw:12607, sw:12615), then gate_drain runs.
  • Trim (cavern_trim, on; trim_days 1): if n > cap, at most one group per depth per day (V7.GRP.trimOne sw:12636-12673):
    • the oldest native first, else the oldest resident;
    • with cavern_trim_gated (on, ruled 2 Oct) and no resident left, the oldest gated DF wave. Its source then reopens (sw:12649-12652).
  • Hold (V7.GRP.cavernHold sw:12723-12768): while any cavern a species lives in is at cap (cavern_hold = 'any'), every natural species with an Animal entry in a cavern and none on the surface or below the caverns (V7.GRP.species sw:12529-12559; cavern_hold_shared off) is held:
    • at V7.GRP.holdLevel = max(2, weight // 10), never above its weight (cavern_hold_mix on, sw:12683-12688);
    • at 1 if the roster itself holds it (V7.GRP.holdFor sw:12691-12700).
    • It releases only the holds it made itself (CACHE.capOwn) and then re-runs CAVERN.apply so the roster's own holds stand (sw:12738-12762).
  • The cavern gate at 5: the hard part is release withholding. It only stops a source that currently has a flagged (gated) group on the map (comment sw:12571-12573, sw:12580).
6.4 Water caps and the tool's own draws (V7.waterTick sw:9280-9345)

  3. A scheduled pass tries at most one body (V7.PASS.WATER_BODIES).
  4. ENGINE.draw (sw:6081-6103):

    • capped by stock and ceiling room;

9. Known gaps, races and suspicious logic

Ordered roughly by impact. "Field" marks a field report this explains.

P1. Field: land overshoot (9.65 vs 9). Nothing ever reduces the land count. There is no land trim; only caverns trim (cavernPass sw:12591; the land block sw:13268-13288 only withholds releases). The status line says so: "paused, N over the cap (placed, DF waves)" (sw:13114). Sources of overshoot, all counted by ENGINE.count (sw:6147):

  • (a) DF draws per border source. Any source whose flagged group died or walked off draws again with no release, and an 8x8 embark has many land sources.
  • (b) Under casts, every land native is adopted and counted whatever natives_land says (sw:12822). The natives_land default of false exists precisely because it is not counted.
  • (c) The apex scheduler places land, water and cavern apex groups against its own cap (cap_calm 1 / cap_savage 2, sw:8861), not the layer cap, and they count (resident, placed, sw:8829-8834).
  • (d) A release at n = cap-1 lets the released source draw again, so the count reaches the cap. Any spontaneous source then overshoots it.
  • (e) Dismissed (turnover) groups count until they physically leave.

P2. Field: the cavern gate cannot hold DF's waves (23-31 groups in cavern 2 vs cap 5).

  • (a) Release withholding stops only a source that currently has a flagged group on the map (sw:12571-12580). A cavern spans many region-tile sources.
  • (b) The hold is a weight, not a gate. Every in-season natural cavern-only species drops to weight // 10 (minimum 2). Per the code's own comments, FREQUENCY is "a comparative weight on a shared budget" (sw:12675-12682). Scaling every species by the same factor keeps their ratios, so it thins arrivals only to the extent that DF's wave rate depends on absolute frequency. The code has no test of that.
  • (c) Species that also live on the surface, in surface water or in the deep are never held (cavern_hold_shared off, s.deep; sw:12728). While the gate holds, their share of the cavern draw rises about 10x.
  • (d) Under casts, resting species sit at restfreq 1 while in-cast species are cut to /10. The gate cuts the cast's advantage over resting species from about 50:1 to about 5:1, so it amplifies cast leaks while a cavern is at cap.
  • (e) The trim removes at most one group per cavern per day (cavern_trim_days 1), at countdown 10. trim_gated empties a gated wave's source, which reopens it (acknowledged at sw:12651-12652).
  • (f) Natives are counted (sw:12577), so the cap can already be exceeded at load.

P3. The trim sends off the season's own cast after a turnover (a bug). cavernPass counts dismissed groups in n (sw:12595-12601). trimOne treats only grp.trimmed as pending, and picks only not grp.dismissed residents (sw:12640-12656). After castTurnover dismisses off-cast cavern groups, n stays over the cap until they have walked out. So the trim sends off the oldest in-cast native or resident group (or an in-cast gated wave) once a day until then. Likely fix: count grp.dismissed as pending in trimOne.

P4. Field: cavern lead time (a species arriving about 28 t after a change). Three parts of the code explain this; the code cannot prevent the first.

  • (a) The change lands at the boundary tick, plus up to 10-120 t of retry while DF has not turned cur_season (sw:4117-4119).
  • (b) DF can deliver a wave whose species it picked before the FREQUENCY change.
  • (c) castTurnover runs once per season stamp (sw:12781). An off-cast group that arrives after the pass that made the turnover, whether a DF wave picked before the change or a FREQUENCY-1 leak (about 15%, sw:3681), is never sent off that season. It also occupies a cavern cap slot.
  • Suggestion: make the turnover a standing rule (any off-cast tracked group, any pass), not a one-shot.

P5. Field: minks drowning loop (water balance x20).

  • (a) Every swimmer enters at the deepest full-water tile. V7.WAT.colTile walks a column from the bottom up and returns the first 7/7 tile (sw:7043-7054; runs store bottom*1024 + top, sw:6548). No check asks whether the species breathes water.
    • MODEL.habitat calls a species "semiaquatic" if it breathes water or if all its raw biomes are water (sw:1901-1902).
    • So an air-breathing semiaquatic with a lake or river feature entry is a Driver B candidate (sw:5978) and is placed on the bed of the deepest column.
  • (b) When those animals die, the census sees an empty predator level, because it counts living units only (WILD.onMap; sw:6313). V7.WAT.mix then boosts the same species: balance up to balance_cap 4 (sw:6417), pyramid ((13+1)/(0+1))^0.5 ≈ 3.7 for a missing C level (sw:6425-6427), pull up to 4 with prey present (sw:6432). The product is clamped to mix.cap = 20 (sw:6438).
  • (c) No record of deaths, drowning or failed survival feeds back. retry_days applies only to empty draws (sw:9322-9325).
  • Suggestion: place air-breathers at the top tile of a column, or on the shore.

P6. The reserve is thrown away when a refund beats the apply (a bug). In applyLive's in-season branch, res[id] = nil runs unconditionally (sw:3049). The reserve is restored only when quantity <= 0. If DF refunds a departing animal to an out-of-season entry in the up to 300 t between the last hold and the change that brings the species back in season, the entry is > 0. The stored reserve (possibly thousands, e.g. "cavies 5,191", sw:2977-2978) is then discarded and the entry keeps only the refund. The same pattern is in exhaustCheck (sw:3175-3181) and spillCheck (sw:7406-7411).

  • Suggestion: add the reserve to the live quantity instead of discarding it.

P7. A cavern raw changed between daily passes is not re-asserted. CAVERN.apply runs at the turn and daily only (sw:4107, sw:4142). Two cases:

  • A cavern stand-in (exhaustion) stays held up to a day.
  • With restfreq 0, when the gate newly holds a resting natural species it writes holdFor = 1 over the 0 (sw:12695-12696, sw:12755), until the next daily pass.

P8. A season turn that errors in the daily job kills rotation for the season. tick calls V7.ST.turn without a pcall (sw:4139); V7.ST.now and the groups fallback do pcall it. An error trips the daily job's fuse, which cancels it: no daily holds, no CAVERN.apply, no timer, no registry rescan, no apex scheduler. Meanwhile lastSeason was already set as the turn's first statement (sw:4094), so the groups-pass fallback will not retry. appliedStamp was never set either, so exhaustion is off all season.

P9. Disable leaves the world held. No disable, off, All off or unload path restores population quantities or extinct flags (section 7.3). Every out-of-season, inactive or resting entry stays at 0 and extinct in the saved world after disable or off; the stock is only in the tool's reserve record. Raws reload clean; entries do not.

P10. The cast season filter depends on classification timing.

  • castPool and castReplaceOk read e.noSeason from the memoized classify. With seasons_own, V7.apply clears the NO_<season> caste flags in the raws.
  • Any path that empties CACHE.class while those writes stand makes the next buildPool reclassify with the flags already cleared: undo (UNDO.after sw:15985, then enableSched builds the pool before V7.apply restores, sw:15932), extinct toggles (sw:14381), the v7 verb (sw:18683), and roster set ap_vermin_mass (sw:9267).
  • After that, noSeason reads all false for the rest of the world session. Later castYear or cast rebuild calls cast species into seasons their raws forbid, and the filter is silently gone.

P11. CACHE.castTurnStamp is never dropped (absent from CACHE.drop sw:17078-17097). The comment promises "once after load" (sw:12775-12776). But loading a save, or another fort, in the same DFHack session and the same game year and season skips the turnover entirely, and off-cast natives adopted at load stay all season. CACHE.grpAdoptAt and CACHE.watSpillAt are not dropped either (milder).

P12. Turnover-dismissed groups bias the clock. V7.GRP.observe skips trimmed groups but not dismissed ones (sw:12372). Every season change feeds truncated stays into W, which pulls W down and shortens release gaps.

P13. A pattern table that spans a season change can starve the new season. The follow pattern, a coupling or a call snapshots every land entry and restores it later (PATTERN.openOnly sw:10272-10299, endCoupling sw:10535-10558). If the restore comes after the season's applyLive, newly in-season species go back to the snapshot's 0. Nothing refills them until the next change (holdOutOfSeason only zeroes), and exhaustCheck then calls them exhausted and promotes stand-ins. In the other direction the restore re-opens out-of-season entries until the next hold (≤ 300 t). The default steady pattern with coupling parked avoids this.

P14. The reserve is keyed by vector position (tostring(r.i), sw:3038, sw:3081) with no race recorded. If world.populations.all is reordered or grows mid-vector (Add invasive writes the region master and needs a reload, sw:505-506), a remembered stock can be handed to another species' entry. applyLive checks the current row's race, but not that the reserve belongs to it. The coupling snapshot g.exclusion.snapshot has the same problem.

P15. enable turns on the deep layer, contradicting R60. PANEL.fortOn sets every Panel switch, including deep (sw:16011, sw:16102). This has three effects:

  • ROSTER.normalize in enableSched (not cast-aware; only fortOn checks castOn, sw:16109 vs sw:15933) gives deep species one default season each, outside the casts;
  • holdOutOfSeason and CAVERN.apply then rotate magma-sea species;
  • under casts, CAVERN.restLevel applies to held deep species too (sw:3753), so restfreq 0 can zero a deep raw.

The R60 comments ("the deep is never touched", sw:12319, sw:12590, sw:4898-4900) hold only for groups and placement.

P16. Water caps and the mix are per body type, not per body. Two lakes share water:lake's count, cap, clock and census (sw:9294, sw:6318-6331). Cavern pools get no tool draws at all (sw:695).

P17. Send-off relies on DF's leave path. No teleport or removal. A dismissed school in an enclosed lake or pool has no edge to walk to; nothing in the code checks that it ever leaves, and it counts against the body's cap until it does.

P18. Outside casts, flagged fish in a water feature are never tracked. gatedLayer drops feature_idx ~= -1 (sw:4354), and adoptNatives takes flagged units only under casts (sw:12815). With casts off these units "fell between the two" (sw:12809-12811): never counted and never led.

P19. Exhaustion borrows are not given back on disable (only spill is, sw:15972). A borrowed season stays in assign until the next season stamp. Low impact because exhaustRoll catches it later.

P20. Small code-comment errors.

  • CAVERN.restLevel's comment says the pool's vermin test "reads only GROUNDER, ROTTER and SOIL" (sw:3690-3691). classify also reads VERMIN_FISH, VERMIN_EATER and SMALL_RACE (sw:1801-1802).
  • exhaustCheck's comment still says the apply is "in the daily tick" (sw:3125). Since 34a3a4b it is usually the timer.

M3Food web construction

Animals act only on what the ecology pass writes: every 3,000 ticks it pairs each armed predator with each non-armed wild animal in its realm, checks reach, the raptor size cap, the pack-mass floor and (under casts) the season cast's edge list, and writes PREDATOR_OR_PREY both ways; then it sweeps every wild-on-wild pair it did not allow. Four different edge rules exist in the code, and only this one moves animals.

The four edge rules

  • The mass rule (eats(), with the ×1.5 LARGE_PREDATOR factor): the old matrix roster, coupling, the water pull and the web page's food-web view.
  • The tier rule: the builder and the casts (higher tier, reach, shared cavern depth; pack bonus as a pick weight).
  • The relation write's filter: armed × non-armed, reach, size cap, pack floor, cast edges. The only rule DF acts on.
  • The vermin wiring: GOBBLE_VERMIN_CLASS through the ten SWV_* classes.

Hunters and the rest

Skill profiles on DF's 0-20 scale with a rust floor (confirmed on the rig), packs inferred within 10 tiles, raptors armed, cohesion and leaders (the largest adult male), scavenging at a natural cadence (confirmed), curious thieves reformed after one theft, irruption v2 in the caverns. Parked by default and forced off at every load: the nudge, coupling, vermin foraging, the raptor stoop, bear fishing and panic flight.

With the seasons

Under casts the writes are limited to the current season's edges, groups sent off at a change are dismissed once a season, and their wild pairs are swept. So the web does change with the season, but only along edges grown into the cast.

Most consequential findings. enable turns "livestock as prey" on, so every armed predator in every realm is related to the fort's animals, and caravan animals always are (P1). Under casts no cross-layer pair is ever written: no alligator × deer, osprey × fish or otter × land prey, and bears cannot fish (P2). The cast's edges do not match who hunts: BENIGN members (badger, fox, stoat, sperm whale) fill slots but never hunt, and apex × mesopredator edges are never written because armed predators are never targets (P3). Parked features cannot be un-parked by their verbs (P4). Thieves sent off at a change are reformed and stay (P5). Disable leaves relation cells, follow links and fishing and panic paths in place (P6).

The field reports read the same way from the code: the stoop adds nothing because it is parked and acts only on pairs already written; panic shows no scatter because it is parked; VF1 never scored a meal because forage is parked and a meal counts only when a whole vermin object vanishes.

0. Short version

  • The relation write is the only lever that makes wild animals hunt. Everything else either decides which species are on the map, or moves animals so they meet.
  • The code has four edge rules that do not agree. (1) A mass rule eats(). (2) A tier rule ROSTER.linkable / ROSTER.castEats, used by the builder and the season casts. (3) The relation write's own filter, ecoWrite. (4) The vermin gobble wiring. The web companion draws a fifth picture: eats() plus a habitat table.
  • Who the write relates. Each pass it pairs every armed predator (any food-web predator that is not wholly BENIGN, plus raptors and cavern civ races) with every non-armed wild animal in the same realm. A pair must pass a reach test, the raptor size cap, the pack-mass floor and, when casts are on, the season cast's edge list. The cell is written PREDATOR_OR_PREY in both directions.
  • Season casts (v7.3, on by default) narrow the web to the cast's edges. Cast edges are built one layer at a time and only point down a tier. So with casts on:
    • no cross-layer predation is ever written (alligator × deer, bear × fish, osprey × fish);
    • no apex × mesopredator pair is written;
    • every cast edge whose predator is BENIGN (badger, fox, stoat, sperm whale…) is a dead edge.
  • Parking (v7.3). V7.parkNudges forces nudge, coupling, vermin foraging, raptor stoop, the fishers master switch and panic off at every config load. No verb un-parks them, and the verbs that turn them on are reverted at the next reload. In some verbs that reload is immediate.
  • enable turns "Livestock as prey" ON. PANEL.fortOn sets every non-parked Panel switch, opt-ins included. ecology.livestock = true then relates every armed predator in every realm, cavern 1–3 included, to the fort's livestock and pets. It also bypasses the v7.domestic guild rule.
  • Nothing undoes the relation cells. Disable and All off leave every written PREDATOR_OR_PREY cell in DF's table. They also leave cohesion follow links, fishing paths and panic paths in place.

3. The ecology pass

3.1 Schedule and gate
  • scheduleGroups (SW:13330) registers ecologyJob every ecology.cadence ticks (3,000) only if ecology.enabled at schedule time.
  • ecologyJob (SW:9570–9578) returns unless cfg.enabled and cfg.groups.enabled and cfg.ecology.enabled. So turning groups off stops ecology too (disableGroups cancels the job, SW:13342).
  • Each pass runs ecologyRun (SW:5468–5510), then V7.soloUnits() (skills onto new units), then saves the groups state.
  • Order inside ecologyRun:
    1. ecoClearDeparted (SW:5024): every slot the tool recorded whose unit died, left or was re-slotted gets its whole row and column set to NONE (-1). A slot DF has given to a non-natural unit is skipped (fb_safe).
    2. ecoPredatorSet (SW:4972): the armed set by token, rebuilt per CACHE.ver.
    3. Unit walk over units.active: a unit is a predator if ecoIsPredator (on map, natural, a realm, in the armed set); otherwise a target if ecoIsTarget.
    4. With civ_prey on, armed civ-race units are added to the target list as well (wtargets, SW:5485–5490).
    5. ecoWrite (§3.4).
    6. V7.sweep (§3.5).
    7. V7.H.stoop and V7.H.fish (§4.3, §4.4), each in a pcall.
    8. ecoNudge if ecology.nudge (§3.6).
    9. A ledger line, and g.ecology.last for status.
3.2 Who is armed (ecoArmed, SW:4958–4971)

An entry on the land, water or cavern layer, not locked, is armed if any of these holds:

  • civ race: civ and v7.civ_hunt and guild in AL/AW/ML/MW/RP and not benign;
  • raptor: guild RP, not civ, hunters.raptors on. RP castes have BENIGN cleared by V7.apply (SW:13837–13839);
  • ordinary: cat == 'predator' and not benign.

Deep (magma sea, underworld) entries are never armed. The curated MODEL.ROLE predators (orca) count as not benign (SW:1821), and CURIOUS.apply clears their BENIGN castes while ecology is on (SW:13464–13476).

Not armed: every BENIGN predator-by-diet that is not RP and not in MODEL.ROLE. In vanilla 53.16 that includes BADGER, FOX, STOAT, MONGOOSE, WOLVERINE, CAT, SPERM_WHALE, BIRD_SWALLOW_CAVE_GIANT and several extinct species (raw read). Their prey are never related to them.

3.3 Targets (ecoIsTarget, SW:4994–5005)
  • Never a target: dead or inactive units, non-natural units (fb_safe), citizens, and non-animal merchants.
  • Wild units are targets if they have a realm, are not a sponge, and are not of an armed species. So armed predators never become prey of each other (except civ races under civ_prey).
  • Tame own-civ animals (V7.isDomestic) are targets only if ecology.livestock or v7.domestic.
  • Any other animal (caravan pack animals, visitor mounts, invaders' beasts) is always a target.

3.5 The sweep (V7.sweep, SW:5252–5325; v7.sweep default on)
  • After the write, every PREDATOR_OR_PREY cell (either direction) between two managed wild units whose pair was not allowed this pass is set to NONE.
  • "Managed wild" is V7.managedWild, SW:5243: alive, active, wild, not tame/citizen/merchant, natural, a non-locked pool species, not undead.
  • It walks O(m²) in a 20 ms slice and resumes where it stopped.
  • It counts refought: a pair DF wrote back within 4 passes.
  • It also clears any non-BENIGN unit's PREDATOR_OR_PREY cell onto a sponge (SW:5300–5311).
  • This is what makes casts and resting stick: DF's own wild-on-wild writes between managed species are removed every pass. Fort-side cells (tame, citizens, caravans) are never touched.
3.6 The nudge (ecoNudge, SW:5436–5466; ecology.nudge default false and parked)
  • A predator farther than far_tiles (40) from its nearest same-realm target for far_ticks (3,000) is teleported to a ground tile, or a water tile for a water creature, within radius (6) of that target, at the target's z (ecoPlaceNear, SW:5408).
  • Fix e494d33 (SW:5442–5451): a non-wild target counts as realm land, so a cavern predator is never moved up to livestock.
  • The relation itself may still be written across realms (SW:5158).
  • The nudge's target list is targets, not the allowed pairs (see §10.A6).

    • target: the nearest allowed target that is ground-living, not swimming, within 40 tiles and ≤ 20 z;

4.4 Bears fishing and polar bears (V7.H.fish, SW:9986–10048; v7.fishers default true, parked)

For each predator on fisher_list with an allowed aquatic target within fish.range (40) and ±6 z, rate-limited by fish.cooldown (1,200; halved in fish.run 'SuAu'):

  1. Find a shore pair G/W near the bear, else near the fish (V7.H.bankPair, SW:~9905). This includes the ramp-bank case (rampBank).
  2. Bear at G, or bear able to walk a straight line to it: up to lure (2; +1 in the run) fish are teleported into the water tiles beside G (V7.H.lure).
  3. Otherwise the bear gets a straight walk, or DF's pathfinder (setPathGoal SeekStation). After fish.wait (600) it gets one hop.
  4. Polar bear (fish.polar) with no shore pair near it: goTo with water=true, i.e. a path to the fish, then a hop into water beside it.

V7.apply also writes CAN_SWIM_INNATE and CAN_SWIM on listed fishers, and CAN_BREATHE_WATER only with fish_breathe (SW:13873–13881).

The relation that the fishing needs exists only through the fisher exception at SW:5182. That exception is gated by the same v7.fishers switch, and with casts on it is then cut by the cast-edge test (SW:5183; §10.A3).

4.6 Cohesion, leaders, panic
  • Label. cohesionLabel (SW:10442–10459) reads MODEL.cohesionOf (SW:9628: override → cluster-of-one solitary → school → flock → pack → herd). A raws-solitary species in a tracked group falls back to school/flock/pack/herd. Cetaceans are pod. Immobile species are never led.
  • Leader (V7.leaderOf, SW:10486–10514, v7.leader_male on): the largest adult male; a water group needs a wet leader. No adult male means no leader (grp.unled). With leader_male off, the lowest id leads.
  • Cohere (V7.GRP.cohere, SW:10520–10569, each groups pass of 300 ticks):
    • followers get following = leader, owner_type = PACK_LEADER, follow_distance = follow_<label>;
    • drawn/placed groups have their leave countdowns synced to the leader's once.
  • Leader lost (V7.GRP.leaderLost, SW:12429–12447): the leader is missing from the live members (dead or inactive). Then:
    • cohesion is cleared and grp.leader_lost set, so the group stays unled (unless reelect_after_panic);
    • if groups.panic is on (default true, parked) and the label is herd/pack/school/pod (flock only with panic_flocks), a panic starts for panic_days (2).
  • Panic step (V7.GRP.panicStep, SW:12493–12530): each pass, members on the leader's z within panic_tiles (12) of where it fell get a straight walk of ≤ 40 steps away from the spot (scatter), or toward the nearest edge (edge). Fliers are skipped (walkToward, SW:12469).
4.7 Curious beasts (R30 reform)

  • clears the unit's enemy.caste_flags curious bits;

5. Scavenging, vermin foraging, remains

5.1 Scavenging (SCAV, SW:14766–15452; scavenge.enabled default false, opt-in, but enable turns it on)
  • Scavengers (SCAV.kind): BONECARN, CURIOUS_BEAST_EATER, SCAV.TEXT, or a swimmer scavenger. Never humanoid races. Wild and natural units only, ≤ 60 a pass, never the deep layer.
  • Remains (SCAV.edible, SW:14906; naturalRemains): CORPSE or CORPSEPIECE items that are
    • loose, not forbidden, not in a job or building (so not on a stockpile);
    • not a historical figure's;
    • not a humanoid race;
    • not a citizen's, own-civ or tame unit's;
    • of a natural or unclassified race.
  • Natural cadence (SCAV.runNatural, SW:15183, every cadence 200 ticks):
    • Discovery delay. Each remains gets a log-normal delay at first sight: medians small 18 h / medium 10 h / large 6 h, σ 0.6, clamped 2–96 h.
    • Perception. A scavenger notices it only after delay + distance × scent_ticks (10). Range is land 20, flier 40, swim 16 tiles; with scav_mapwide off it is capped at radius 20.
    • Approach. A straight walk on one level, re-issued every 400 ticks (SCAV.walk). Swimmers hop. Anyone that has not closed 2 tiles in hop_wait (600) hops hop_tiles (8), never from beyond hop_max (30), with hops_day 24 and moves_day 60 map-wide.
    • Eating. eat_rate (0.05 × own mass per hour), up to gorge (0.25 × own mass) a visit, then sated for 12 h. At most feeders (3) at once; a smaller species defers to a heavier one.
    • Deletion. The remains is deleted (dfhack.items.remove, SW:15086) only when its mass is gone. Mass is the race's adult size, or piece_share 0.15 of it for a piece.
  • The legacy v7.0 sweep is kept behind scavenge.natural = false (SCAV.runLegacy).
  • Discovery clocks live in CACHE.scav7 (per session). Attribution stats persist in site data.
5.2 Vermin foraging (VERMIN.FORAGE, SW:15453–15855; vermin_eat.forage default true, parked)
  • Cadence. A 100-tick step job plus a draw pass every cadence (1,200).
  • Index (VERMIN.index, SW:15553–15609):
    • read the vermin objects (world.event.vermin) and bucket loose ones by 16×16 block;
    • colonies are weak attractors (colony 0.25);
    • visible == false objects are not targets;
    • the read stops at a 25 ms deadline (idx.partial).
  • Eligible eaters (VERMIN.forager, SW:15661): wild, natural, on the map, not deep, not tame, idle, not a scavenger on a job, and whose caste carries any gobble class or creature.
  • Draw (VERMIN.forageOne): up to budget (8) eaters a pass, each with chance 0.5, cooldown 3,600. Each picks a vermin within radius (20) that its live gobble classes match, weighted by nearness, amount and diversity. It gets a straight path (walk/swim/fly) to within REACH 2. No teleport.
  • Outcome (VERMIN.forageResolve, SW:15740–15762), at each draw pass:
    • eaten: the target object id vanished and the forager had arrived;
    • gone: vanished, not arrived;
    • missed: arrived, still there at EXPIRE 1,500;
    • expired: never arrived.
  • DF's own gobble does the eating; the tool only walks the eater.
5.3 Corpses
  • Nothing in DF eats remains (header, SW:14769). Only SCAV deletes them.
  • Kills made by related predators leave corpses that persist until a scavenger finishes them, or forever with scavenging off.

6. Layer-specific

6.1 Water
  • Realm. Water-layer units are in the land realm (ecoRealm, SW:4946). Who reaches whom is REACH plus sharesWater.
  • Draw community weights (V7.WAT.mix, SW:6394–6439), on top of the FREQUENCY base, for each candidate in a body:
    • balance: ((want+0.02)/(have+0.02))^balance, clamped 1/4–4;
    • feed: prey with predators present, 1 + 0.5 × predator groups, ≤ 4;
    • pyramid: ((target%+1)/(share%+1))^0.5, clamped 0.25–4;
    • apex limit: weight 0 at apex_max (1) apex groups;
    • pull: below;
    • the product is clamped to mix.cap (20).
  • Pelagic pull (ENGINE.weight, SW:6046–6059; mix pull term SW:6430–6434):
    • A predator with prey of it swimming in the body (V7.WAT.ctxFor, SW:6356–6372, using the mass rule V7.WAT.eats at SW:6362/6366) is weighted 1 + k × prey mass / own group mass, between 1.5 and 4.
    • A pelagic predator (> pelagic_ref 1,000,000 cm³, ocean) with prey present loses the shallow-map floor (lift_floor). Without prey its weight is cut to pelagic_floor 0.1 on a shallow map.
  • Stranding guard: removed in v7.1-place (docs/v7.1/water.md:3–7, SW:6156–6157).
    • Every swimmer enters by V7.WAT.entry: ocean/river edge columns, else the lake centroid.
    • The entry tile is the lowest free tile at 7/7 (V7.WAT.colTile, SW:7043–7055).
    • Air breathers are not told apart (§10.C1).
  • Curated aquatic apexes (aquatic_apex, §2.7) set roster slots and pull level 'A'. They do not arm a BENIGN species: SPERM_WHALE and GIANT_SPERM_WHALE are curated 'pelagic' but stay unarmed.
  • Fishers. §4.4.
6.2 Caverns

10. Gaps, dead code, contradictions, suspicious logic

A. Behaviour-level problems (highest impact first)

A1. enable turns livestock-as-prey on for every armed predator in every realm.

  • PANEL.fortOn (SW:16101–16102) sets every Panel switch not in V7.PARKED_SWITCHES = {nudge, coupling, forage} (SW:1070). livestock is not in that set (opt-in only matters for "all on").
  • With ecology.livestock true, dom[] stays empty (SW:5094), so the v7.domestic guild restriction is bypassed. Every armed predator, cavern depth 1–3 included, is related to the fort's tame animals across realms (SW:5158).
  • The user's startup runs enable on map load (memory: user-df-startup-config), so this is the live configuration.
  • The nudge fix stops the teleport. Nothing stops the cross-realm relation. Whether DF's AI paths a related cavern predator up to its target is unmeasured; it would reproduce the test-item-1 outcome without any teleport.
  • Caravan pack animals and visitor mounts are targets of every armed predator in every realm regardless of any switch (SW:5004).

A2. With casts on, cross-layer predation is never written.

  • ROSTER.castGrow builds edges inside one layer's partitions only (ROSTER.castPool SW:8391; edges SW:8562–8564). Land = land + flying; water = water; cavern = cavern.
  • ecoWrite then drops every wild pair not in the edge set (SW:5183).
  • Blocked: alligator/crocodile (water roster) × deer (land); land carnivores × water-layer shore animals; waterbirds and ospreys × fish; otters × land prey. The code comment at SW:4945 cites ECO W1L: alligators killed 5 of 8 deer from the water.

A3. With casts on, bears never fish.

  • The fisher exception (SW:5182) sets sp = true for land × aquatic, but the very next line requires a cast edge (SW:5183).
  • Cast edges come from castEats → MODEL.reaches, which forbids land → aquatic (REACH).
  • So the bear × fish pair is never allowed, V7.H.fish finds no allow (SW:10010), and the fishing lever is inert even if un-parked. Polar bears in water are inert the same way.

A4. Cast edges include dead edges and omit live ones.

  • Dead edges. castEats and linkable never check BENIGN. A cast can seat BADGER, FOX, STOAT, MONGOOSE, WOLVERINE or CAT in the meso slot, or SPERM_WHALE in the pelagic apex slot (all BENIGN in all castes in vanilla 53.16, raw read). Each such member's prey edges are never written (ecoArmed, SW:4970). The desk's "every cast one connected web" is a tier graph, not the realised hunting graph.
  • Omitted edges. Tier-down edges APX → ML/MW/RP exist in the cast, but ecoIsTarget excludes every armed species from the targets (SW:5000). So apex × mesopredator is never written, and intra-guild predation is impossible except for civ races under civ_prey.
  • Irruption civ waves of a non-cast race get no wild-wild relations with cavern fauna. Their effect is only through the tokens and agitation.

A5. CURIOUS reform re-keeps every thief that is sent off.

  • castTurnover → dismissGroup zeroes leave_countdown (SW:10596). So do sendoff, call and cavern trim.
  • CURIOUS.pass (every 50 ticks, reform on, not parked) treats a thief-species unit with countdown 0 as a fresh thief. It sets a new 20k–30k countdown and repeats up to 10 times (SW:13653, 13622–13636).
  • With need_theft off (default) no theft sign is needed (SW:13583).
  • Result: bears and raccoons sent off at a season turnover stay for up to ~11 × 25,000 ticks, as resting species on the map. A natural end of stay is caught the same way.

A6. The nudge ignores the allowed pairs.

  • ecoNudge picks the nearest same-realm target from the full targets list (SW:5441–5452), not CACHE.ecoAllow.
  • A wolf can be teleported beside a fish, a shark, an off-cast species or a too-big prey, at the target's z. Only the tile type (ground or water by the mover) is checked.
  • It also ignores the civ-prey extra targets. It is parked, but it is exposed as a Panel switch.

A7. Parking makes the stoop, fishing, forage, nudge and panic switches unreliable.

  • V7.parkNudges runs on every config load (SW:1057), and there is no verb for parked.
  • hunters stoop on saves and then reloads the config at once (reapply: CACHE.cfg = nil; loadConfig(), SW:10093–10094), so it is parked again before any pass runs. The same holds for v7 fishers on (SW:18683).
  • vermin forage on and groups panic on survive until the next reload: any of the 9 CACHE.cfg = nil sites, a map load, or even vermin forage now (SW:18219–18222).
  • The Panel/controls show these as normal switches.

A8. The memo and the sweep can disagree, leaving a pair unwritten for up to 8 passes.

  • When a pair is written, CACHE.ecoDone[slots] = ids.
  • If on a later pass the pair is not allowed, the sweep clears the cell (SW:5285–5288) but does not clear the memo. The pair can lose its allowance because the inferred pack shrank under pack_floor, a packmate stepped out of pack_radius, or the cast changed.
  • When it is allowed again, done[k] == pid counts it as written (SW:5212) and it stays empty until the 8th-pass refresh (≈ 24,000 ticks).
  • The pack-floor check is per pass and per pair (SW:5187–5189), so pairs near the floor can flicker.

A9. The realm test inside MODEL.reaches uses the entry's layer, not the unit's.

  • byTok[token] is the first pool entry of a species (SW:4979). The memo test MODEL.reaches(ea, eb) compares (e.layer=='cavern') ~= (p.layer=='cavern') (SW:2569).
  • A species with both surface and cavern entries (V7.GRP.species marks these shared) can therefore be refused against same-cavern prey when its first entry is the land one, or the reverse.
  • The unit-level realm test already ran (SW:5158). Needs a rig check on a shared species.

A10. Wild units with no pool entry bypass every test.

  • A wild unit whose species has no entry (T.eb[i] == false) gets ok = true with no reach, cast, raptor or floor test (SW:5195).
  • Examples: placed from another region, an invasive, some apex-scheduler placements.
  • Such units are also outside the sweep's managed set, and are recorded as …|DOMESTIC (SW:5219).

A11. The skill profile can be promoted by the tool's own write.

  • V7.apply writes AMBUSHPREDATOR on solitary castes (SW:13846).
  • roster set ap_vermin_mass resets CACHE.class while those writes are live and does not re-run V7.apply (SW:9267). Classification then reads ambush=true for solitary mesopredators.
  • At the next V7.apply, the restore runs but the class cache is stale. profileOf promotes them to "solitary apex" (15 instead of 12) and takeOf gains ×1.5. This lasts until world unload.

A12. Undo gaps.

  • Relation cells are not undone on disable or All off (§8).
  • Cohesion follow links (PACK_LEADER) are not cleared on disable or All off: there is no clearCohesion in disableSched or PANEL.restoreAll.
  • Fishing goTo paths and panic walkToward paths have no release.
  • The sponge sweep clears any non-BENIGN unit's cell onto a sponge, citizens and forgotten beasts included (SW:5300–5308). This is a small exception to the fb_safe promise at SW:2236.
B. Dead or ineffective code
  • B1. eats(), takeOf/needOf, k_large (×1.5) and preference never reach the relation write. They drive only the matrix roster, co-align, coupling, the water pull/feed, the species page and the web graph. PRED comment (SW:2496–2515) describes a "can hunt" rule that the animals do not follow.
  • B2. The aquatic apex list does not arm. SPERM_WHALE and GIANT_SPERM_WHALE are curated pelagic apexes (SW:7576–7577) and are pulled in by prey (V7.WAT.eats → eats, SW:6263). They are BENIGN, not in MODEL.ROLE, and so never hunt (SW:4970).
  • B3. Builder edges and the GOBBLE edge table are selection or data only, except VERMIN.rosterRows. That turns builder 'write' edges into gobble class writes, using a random vermin draw per build (SW:8019–8024).
  • B4. The 'snake' gobble rule never fires. VERMIN.legless (SW:14531–14543) reads gi[i].name where gait_info[i] is a vector of gaits; the struct has no name either. The pcall therefore fails and returns false. docs/v7.1/vermin.md:163–164 still lists snakes as SWV_SMALL_MAMMAL and SWV_HERP eaters.

C. Field reports checked against the code

C1. Minks drowning loop.

  • Classification. Vanilla MINK is BENIGN, has no diet token, and has only ANY_TEMPERATE_LAKE/RIVER biomes, with no AMPHIBIOUS or CAN_BREATHE_WATER (raw read). MODEL.habitat makes it semiaquatic (water-only biomes, SW:1902), guild SH, role prey, and a water-layer entry.
  • Draw. ENGINE.candidates takes any water-layer entry regardless of habitat (SW:5978).
  • Placement. V7.WAT.colTile puts every water entrant on the lowest free 7/7 tile of a column (SW:7043–7055). An air-breather starts submerged. The stranding guard that regrounded drowning air-breathers was removed in v7.1-place (docs/v7.1/water.md:3–7, 34).
  • Loop. The minks die, so have = 0. V7.WAT.mix then rewards the absent species: balance up to ×4, pyramid H up to ×4, feed up to ×4, product clamped at mix.cap 20 (SW:6411–6438). The next draw picks minks again.
  • Fix shape. Separate air-breathing semiaquatic entrants (surface tile or shore), or exclude them from Driver B.
  • Related: stuckSweep (SW:10658–10677) regrounds any tracked non-flier unit, fish included, that has not moved for 5,000 ticks.

C2. Stoop adds nothing over the ecology write. Reasons in the code:

  1. At HEAD the stoop is parked off and cannot be turned on persistently (A7). If the report predates 7 Oct the next points apply.
  2. It only acts on pairs the write already allowed (SW:9838), so it adds proximity, never a relation.
  3. The dose is small. It rides the 3,000-tick ecology pass, at a 60% chance, with a 2,400 cooldown (shorter than the cadence), so at most one stoop per raptor per pass and three map-wide.
  4. It places a flier on the ground 1–2 tiles off. Nothing holds it there, and DF's flier AI takes off again; whether a landed raptor's next tick is an attack is unmeasured.
  5. Raptors are capped to prey ≤ 2× their mass. Their prey is the same set the write relates, so with the relation present any meeting would happen without the stoop anyway if DF's AI closes the distance.

C3. Lost-leader panic shows no detectable scatter. Reasons in the code:

  1. groups.panic is parked (SW:1079). With panic off, leaderLost only logs and leaves the group unled (SW:12436–12446).
  2. Panic needs a leader to have existed. Leaders are adult males only (SW:10486–10514); groups without one are unled from the start, so there is nothing to lose.
  3. Only members on the leader's z and within 12 tiles move (SW:12505). The move is one straight walk of ≤ 40 steps per 300-tick pass, toward a target ~12–17 tiles away. Fliers are skipped, and DF re-plans a wild roamer quickly.
  4. After the panic the group stays unled (no reelect), so a control arm with an unled group spreads the same way. The measurable difference is only the ≤ 2-day flight.

C4. Vermin forage (VF1) never recorded a meal. Reasons in the code:

  1. At HEAD forage is parked. vermin forage on holds only until a reload (A7), and vermin forage now itself triggers one.
  2. An "eaten" result needs the target vermin object to vanish completely (not idx.alive[p.vid], SW:15743–15751). A swarm whose amount is decremented by a gobble stays alive and is scored missed.
  3. "Arrived" is measured against the vermin's position at the draw (tx,ty, SW:15705, 15729–15731). Loose vermin move, so an eater can reach the old spot while the vermin is elsewhere.
  4. Resolution happens only at draw passes (1,200 ticks). It is skipped entirely when the index hit its 25 ms deadline (idx.partial, SW:15749). On a map with many vermin objects that can be every pass, and then only missed/expired are possible.
  5. visible == false vermin are never targets (SW:15592).
  6. The walk sets goal SeekStation (SW:15639). If DF's gobble runs only from idle wandering, the forced path may delay it. Unmeasured.

M4On, off, presets and the bundle

One switch nearly exists: enable seasonal-wildlife (or seasonal-wildlife on) turns on the water renderer, saves an autostart, and on a loaded fort switches every Panel switch on (opt-ins included, parked ones excluded), every layer, the extinct corrections and the aquarium, and builds the casts. off reverses it, short of the population stock. What is missing is the switch in the places a player looks, and presets in the sense a player means.

Four "on"s, three "off"s

  • enable seasonal-wildlife = seasonal-wildlife on: everything (above).
  • seasonal-wildlife enable rotation: the old rotation-only enable. Panel All on: restores the saved switches. First open of the window on a new fort: the biome preset roster, not the casts.
  • disable seasonal-wildlife = off: renderer off, autostart cleared, writes put back on the loaded fort. seasonal-wildlife disable: rotation only. Panel All off: jobs off, writes put back, renderer left on.

"Preset" means four things

The biome roster preset, the season casts, the aquarium mod's transparency presets (vanilla, subtle, aquarium, clear, shallows, custom) and the renderer's look, which has no presets and whose settings (tints, palettes, veil) are not saved: they reset at every DF start.

Gaps against "on and off at the flip of a switch, presets ready"

  1. No master on/off in the Panel or on the web page; the page cannot send on, off, cast or preset.
  2. Casts are console-only.
  3. Most Panel and web aquarium controls drive the retired Lua overlay and do nothing while the plugin draws the water.
  4. off acts on the loaded fort only, and leaves population stock held.
  5. The renderer's settings are not persisted; no named look presets.
  6. The browser page is not started by enable.
  7. The ecological summary pages are not in the package.
  8. Version strings read 7.2.0 everywhere; the newest zip (749384b1) predates the casts and the water palettes; help text is stale in places.

(b) Presets

There are four unrelated kinds of "preset". None of them is a named on/off profile for the tool.

1. The biome roster preset: seasonal-wildlife preset (engine:18273-18279; applyPreset 3883-3904)
  • Turns on every layer the map has (ROSTER.presentLayers, 3869).
  • Every unlocked in-embark natural species gets defaultAllow, which mostly means active.
  • Each active species gets one season from the climate matrix (assignFromMatrix).
  • The land pattern is steady, and rotation is on.
  • Its name comes from the embark's biomes (presetName 3857, e.g. "temperate shrubland, ...").

It is applied by:

  • the console preset;
  • the window's first open (gui:625-645);
  • R on the Layers tab;
  • fortOn when casts are off.

The web may not send it (controls:722-723).

Read it back from:

  • each species' why entry, preset: <name>;
  • the ledger build line;
  • the Layers tab status line (gui, "Preset re-applied: ...").
2. v7.3 season casts (engine:8280-8305, verb 18333-18405)
  • Each season has its own roster per layer (land, water, cavern), grown from a high-value seed through its prey and predators, with slot quotas in ROSTER.CAST (8281-8297).
  • ROSTER.CAST_DEF = {enabled=true, carry=0.25, giant=0.2, pack=3, hv_petvalue=200, hv_mass=1e6, salt=0}.
  • cast restfreq 0|1 sets the frequency at which resting cavern species are held. The default is 1; vermin always stay at 1.

Verbs: cast on|off, cast rebuild [SALT], cast status, cast show SEASON [LAYER]. They are console only: no registry row, no Panel section and no web verb.

4. The native renderer's look

There is no preset. Every value is a compiled default, changed only by aquarium <verb> console commands (cpp:6531 onward: tint, deeptint, veil, water unified|df, deep tint|off, waves, surf, shimmer, streaks, falls, bedart, ...). Nothing is persisted: there is no json and no site data in the cpp, so every DF restart returns to the compiled defaults. Read back with aquarium / aquarium status (cpp:6286-6300).

Defaults and readback in general
  • The registry (scripts/seasonal-wildlife-controls.lua) reads every default from the engine's defaultConfig() by path (controls:43, defaultOf 631).
  • The Panel marks a non-default value with * and offers a per-control reset.
  • seasonal-wildlife-controls list [SECTION] / json print value, default and range.
  • Window Alt+R asks "reset roster" or "reset everything". ROSTER.resetAll (engine:3954) copies defaultConfig back, then re-applies the present layers. It does not reset cast/casts, which are not in defaultConfig. It is window-only, not in the registry or on the web.

The run kit (build first)

Today the v7.3 season tooling is a set of scripts with no single driver. One driver, season-run, will do for every run:

  1. Load a backed-up fort by folder (never the original), with a per-chunk timeout.
  2. Receipts first: the fort's casts, settings and the manipulation under test are read back and the subject is proven on the map; any failure aborts the cell (manifest-subject rule).
  3. Record: the in-game recorder (arrivals, departures, attacks, kills, deaths, positions every 100 ticks) and the tool's ledger captured live (its buffer keeps only 300 lines), plus ecology pairs tsv and cast status every 3,000 ticks.
  4. Step in 20,000-tick chunks, saving each (DF hangs about every 40-60k ticks at speed), watering and feeding the dwarves each chunk (rig embarks carry no drink).
  5. Dump and analyse: one JSON per cell (v73_check, f0_compare and the food-web graph), then the season-test page rebuilt and republished.

5. What this means for efficient new runs

  1. Use 2x2 region19 forts. They run about 300 t/s fresh, and a season takes 5-6 min. Take save-backup <fort> preverify for V73_LAKE2, V73_OCEAN2 and V73_BROOK first: none exists, and both harnesses need one.
  2. Restart DF per arm or per fort (process age), with a fresh load per arm (H1). Loads (about 0.5-2.5 min) cost more than short reps.
  3. Record in game (sw-t1-recorder plus a live ledger), not by RPC polling, and check with v73_check.py. Pair arms with f0_compare.py-style scripts. Five reps per arm makes 5x the 12-15 min season runs, so keep reps to one season or less.
  4. Chunk steps at 10k with timeouts, run cx-load sustain each chunk, save a named copy every ~20k if the run is long (the hang trap), and park new save folders afterwards.
  5. Decide the install under test. Either the package (remove the dfhack-config shadow copies, DF stopped) or hot-swapped scripts. Record which one in the provenance; cx-experiment's SW_REPO and the validator's SW_TOOL default to the stale v7.0 checkout.
