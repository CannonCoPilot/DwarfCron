2Build the casts once, at the first switch-on; again only on cast rebuild

All 28 casts (7 layers × 4 seasons) are built together, in one random order seeded by site and year. This is the same builder as the Food Web Explorer, so the same fort gives the same casts. A species may sit in several casts, but each extra cast already holding it makes it 100× less likely. For each cast, the candidates are the species in the candidate list that can live on this layer in this season.
1Apex: one species is drawn for the apex slot. With no true apex among the candidates, a local one is used; with none at all, the largest mid predator is promoted.
  • Group mass = adult size × the mean pack size for a pack hunter (a predator whose group can be more than 1); adult size alone for a solitary one. It is what the predator brings onto the map at once.
  • Weight = √(group mass ÷ the largest group mass among the candidates) × 1.5 for a pack hunter × 0.2 for a giant × 0.01 for each other cast already holding the species. The square root favours big hunters without letting the biggest always win.
Example: a temperate forest's apex candidates (vanilla raws)adult sizegroupgroup massweightchancesavage forest, + giant grizzly

Cougar (solitary)60,000 cm³160,000√0.3 = 0.5518%15%

With the giant grizzly present, the largest group mass is 1,700,000, so the others' square roots shrink (grizzly √0.118 = 0.34, wolves 0.34 × 1.5 = 0.51, cougar 0.19), but the giant's ×0.2 keeps it at 16%. If the wolf already heads another season's ground cast, its weight drops to 1.5 × 0.01 = 0.015: then grizzly 64%, cougar 35%, wolf 1%. That is how the four seasons of one layer end up with different apexes.

2Seed: one high-value species into the slot with the most room. Flying seeds follow the fort's surroundings, from the first rung that has a candidate down: evil flier → good flier → savage with extinct species → savage flier (animal person or giant bug) → raptor.
3Grow on the food web (New Rules), until every slot is full or nothing fits:
  • Take the free candidates that eat, or are eaten by, a member already in.
  • Among those, prefer the slot with the most room left, then pick by weight.
  • Weights: pack predators ×3 · ×0.01 for each other cast already holding the species · giants · animal people · cavern depth fit ("weirdness").
4Unfilled slots are recorded ("none in season", "all candidates used in other slots").
5Caverns: food-web guarantee: a predator and its prey are forced in.
6Bonus predator: a giant herbivore that nothing in the cast eats gets one of its eaters in an extra seat.
7Vermin: what the members eat, one per vermin class first, then any.
8Cross-layer links: who eats whom between layers this season (a bird taking fish, a ground predator taking a shore animal).
9Then:
  • The player's pins override the build.
  • FREQUENCY ladder: every cast member gets one number, 1–100, that becomes its FREQUENCY, DF's odds for picking it. This is the step that most decides how often each species shows up; it has its own panel, 2b.
  • Entries are made for any member that has none.

2bThe FREQUENCY ladder built with the casts; written into the raws every day and at each season change

What it is. FREQUENCY is a number in each creature's raws that DF uses as its odds when it picks a species for a wave. When a wave is due, DF compares the FREQUENCY of every species that has an open entry, and a species with 60 is picked 3 times as often as one with 20. The tool rewrites every cast member's FREQUENCY from one "ladder". It is the step that most decides whether a species shows up far too often or far too rarely.

Where the ladder actsHow
Ground, flyingDF's own waves: each species has one open entry (single source), so its odds are its FREQUENCY ÷ the sum over the season's open members. The tool's own draw (when DF has nothing waiting) takes the least-seen member first, then uses the ladder as its weights.
Water (ocean, lake, river, ponds)DF sends no water waves. The tool's draw starts from the ladder value, then multiplies it by balance, pyramid, pull and feed (panel 5 k).
Caverns 1–3DF's own waves, as on the surface. B1 suggests stock matters more than FREQUENCY there PENDING, which the cavern open stock (30) is meant to offset.
Apex predatorsNot on the ladder. Their own raw FREQUENCY, capped at 5, so DF rarely sends them. The apex scheduler places them instead (its presence targets: land 25, water 20, caverns 70).
Resting speciesNot on the ladder. Surface: held by their stock (entries at 0). Caverns: FREQUENCY 0 (vermin 1).

Why a species ends up far too common or far too rare

1 · Solitary and small rises to the top
Savage marsh: arthropleura 100 (solitary, the smallest land prey, size factor capped ×4), weasel and adder 23. Ocean coast: red panda 100 (the smallest grazer, solitary), and on the flying ladder the barn owl 100, a predator above every prey bird (raven 38, kea 34). Every wave is one animal but takes a whole slot. On the Roofpuzzle fort, hares and barn owls took about 68% of land waves.
2 · Big groups sink
Savage marsh: wild boar 12 (mean group 7.5) against the solitary giant hamster 44 in the same slot; wolf 1 (group 5). Dividing by group size makes herds and packs rare as waves, though each wave brings many animals.
3 · The floor of 1
Anything under 0.5% of the top rounds up to 1. Every very rare species looks the same, and 1 against 100 is 1% of waves. In earlier tests only 26% of cast species at FREQUENCY 1–2 ever arrived, against 75% at 21 or more.
4 · The whole year sets the scale
A species' number depends on every member of its partition across all four seasons, not on its own season's cast. A season of only big herds and predators is all low numbers. Their ratios still hold, but the floor and rounding blur them.
5 · Apexes are off the ladder
Raw FREQUENCY capped at 5, so DF almost never sends one. They come from the apex scheduler instead, to a presence target.
6 · One raw, several layers
A species living on two layers (a cavern and the surface) has one FREQUENCY, and the higher value wins.
7 · Caverns may not listen
B1 suggests DF's cavern pick follows stock more than FREQUENCY PENDING. That's why flesh balls at FREQUENCY 10 with 395 stock took 54% of cavern 3.
8 · Water and the tool's draws re-weight it
For water the ladder is only the starting weight (balance, pyramid, pull, feed follow). For the tool's land and flying draws, "least seen this season first" comes before it.
9 · Odds only matter when there is room
The ladder decides who fills an open slot. When places sit at their cap (68–89% of the time in B1), departures decide how often anyone comes.
10 · A missing family weight is 5
A slot with no family entry on its ladder gets 5 (step 3). On water, raptors have no entry, so the osprey gets 5 against 2 for the water mid predators and 6 for shore prey. Savage marsh: osprey 30, above carp (15) and sturgeon (13).
11 · The season's seed often comes last
The seed (★ in the season tables) is the species a season's cast is built around, yet the ladder does not favour it. In 30 of the 72 season casts on the three real forts above, the seed has the lowest (or tied lowest) FREQUENCY of its season's non-apex members: lake fort giant fly 1, giant capybara 1 and alligator 1; draltha 7 in cavern 1 on all three forts.

Settings behind it (cast config): the pyramid targets, units on (step 6), biomass guard 0.5, apex raw cap 5, animal-person and giant FREQUENCY multipliers (1 = off).

2Apply the season

3FREQUENCY written (this runs daily too): every open species gets its ladder value (panel 2b). 6Ecology: the food web acting on the map every 3,000 ticks (2.5 days)

1Clear records of animals that have left.
2Who is who on the map
  • Predators: armed species of the candidate list, on the surface or in a cavern (never in water, the magma sea or the underworld).
  • Targets: wild animals in those realms (never sponges); caravan and visitor animals.
  • The fort's livestock ON: prey of land apexes, ground mid predators and cavern predators only, and only when no wild prey is around.
  • Armed civilised races, as prey of cavern apexes ON.
  • A cast predator can be prey of the cast's higher tiers.
3Write: for each predator → target pair that New Rules allows, a PREDATOR_OR_PREY cell in DF's relation table. This is what makes one animal hunt another; only a non-benign attacker acts on it.
4Sweep ON: any such cell between wild animals that New Rules does not allow (DF writes some itself) is reset to none. A cell aimed at a sponge is always cleared.
5Hunters:
  • Hunting skill levels: solo apex 15, solo mid predator 12, pack 10.
  • Pack hunting within 10 tiles ON.
  • Raptors ON; raptor stoops (a dive onto prey) OFF, parked.
  • Bear fishing walks (summer and autumn) OFF, parked.
6Nudge OFF: a predator more than 40 tiles from every target for 3,000 ticks is moved within 6 tiles of one, in its own realm only.

7Fast jobs

Alerts · 10 ticks ON
A fight between wild animals with no person in it doesn't light the fort's alert button. The reports stay in each animal's log.
Curious thieves stay · 50 ticks ON
A curious beast that steals loses the habit and stays, instead of walking off the map with the loot. A species the player marks "resident" keeps it the same way.
Scavenging · 200 ticks OFF
Bone scavengers (and swimmer scavengers, sharks included) find remains they can reach and eat them.
Vermin forage · 100 ticks OFF
Foragers walk to food; whether it was eaten, gone or missed is traced.
Water renderer · every frame
See-through water with creatures under it. It changes nothing in the game, only how it looks.

8What Dwarf Fortress does on its own always, whether the tool is on or not

Gate
One wave per source at a time. While any unit still carries the wave flag, no new wave comes from that source. Each region tile and each cave is a separate source.
Pick
Among open entries on the site tile and its 8 neighbours (the site tile weighs most).
Surface: FREQUENCY × tile weight, per entry.
Caverns: appears to follow stock PENDING
Group size
Random within the raw's CLUSTER_NUMBER, at most the entry's stock. A wave can walk in over several hundred ticks.
Stock
−1 per animal that arrives; +1 refunded to the entry the unit came from when it leaves. Breeding on the map doesn't refill the entry.
Natives
Animals already in the caverns at embark, and young born on the map, carry no wave flag. The tool adopts them into groups.
Life on the map
The animals wander, fight on relation cells, eat, breed, die, and leave when their own countdown runs out.

9Switch off off, disable, All off

Entries
Every held entry gets its reserve back and its extinct flag cleared. The entries the casts created are removed.
Raws
Every raw write is undone: seasons, FREQUENCY, group sizes, the pack and solitary packages, fishers, gobble, hunting flags, curious flags.
On the map
PREDATOR_OR_PREY cells between wild animals are cleared, follow links released, irruption and trim writes put back.
Jobs
Every job stops; the water renderer turns off. off also clears the global autostart in dfhack-config/seasonal-wildlife.json.
