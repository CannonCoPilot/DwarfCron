Translucent water, ocean waves and mist in graphics mode

Translucent water is an ordinary graphics mod: re-point the seven water-depth tiles at a lower-alpha copy of the sheet. Ocean waves need a DFHack overlay. The game still simulates them, but graphics mode has no tile token to draw them with.

Research 1 Oct 2026, read-only on the installed game.
Sources: the free classic build df_53_16_win.zip, the Steam install's data/vanilla/vanilla_environment/graphics, DFHack's structure definitions, and the four Steam Workshop mods you linked.
Prototypes: DwarfCron/data/dfgfx/proto_mods/
1 · Can water be made semi-transparent, like z-level fog and glass floors?
Yes · a raws and PNG mod

Water is drawn as a layer over the floor of the same cell, and the three shallowest depths are already partly transparent. A mod folder that re-binds UNDERWATER_1–UNDERWATER_7 to a lower-alpha sheet shows the floor through ponds, channels and cisterns. It costs nothing at run time.

  1. Recommended: a raws mod re-binding the seven depth tiles and their depth labels. A working prototype exists.
  2. Rivers and brooks have their own animated sheets, which need the same alpha edit.
  3. A DFHack overlay can only tint on top; it cannot reveal what is underneath.

Untested: what shows through deep ocean or lake water with no floor in the cell. Lower z-levels under fog, or black? That needs one look in a test world.

2 · Can we add the ocean waves and mist that ASCII mode shows?
Mist: a PNG edit · Waves: a DFHack overlay

The rumour holds up. The executable carries the ocean-wave and sea-foam objects, and DFHack's structure definitions name them. But graphics mode's 10 FLOW_* tokens don't include either, so no sprite exists to re-bind. Mist does have a token, FLOW_WATER_MIST, and can be restyled with a PNG edit.

  1. Mist: edit the mist column of event_flows.png through a mod folder.
  2. Waves: a script-only DFHack overlay, built like the Rain Visuals mod. It reads wave fronts and wave/foam flows in the visible blocks a few times a second and paints animated translucent sprites there. A prototype passes a syntax check but has never run.
  3. A C++ plugin or an exe patch could draw waves in the right layer. Neither is recommended.

Fog and glass are translucent because their sprites carry alpha, and the mods that change them simply re-bind the token to a new sheet. Water works the same way: DF's map is drawn floor first, then the liquid layer on top in the same cell. The shallow tiles' existing alpha only makes sense in that order. That's read from DFHack's structure definitions, not yet screen-tested.

EvidenceStatus

The author of the Flowing Water mod hit the same wall: "can't fix the invisible waves at the beach. If they release tokens for the waves, I'll animate them too."

Prototype 1 · translucent_water

A complete mod folder: info.txt, a tile page and the graphics raws. The sheet liquids_translucent.png is vanilla's with alpha 150, 140, 130, 120, 110, 100, 90 for depths 7 to 1. It was built by make_translucent_water.py, so other alpha curves are a one-line change.

Prototype 2 · ocean_waves_overlay

A script-only DFHack mod: scripts_modactive/ocean-waves.lua plus an 8-frame × 2-row sprite sheet. Row one is the wave crest; row two is foam and mist. The overlay rescans 4 times a second: wave fronts on the current level, plus OceanWave, SeaFoam and Mist flows in the visible blocks. It then paints the matching frame with renderMapOverlay, keeping the tile underneath.

Never run in DF. Before it can, it needs three things checked against DFHack 53.16-r1.1: the renderMapOverlay callback's return shape, getModSourcePath('ocean_waves_overlay') matching the mod ID, and whether texture handles survive a world reload.

Testing it on the rig

  1. Copy translucent_water into the Steam mods folder on a throwaway copy of the OCEAN2 and LAKE saves, enable it at world load, and screenshot a channel, a pond and open ocean. This settles the open question: is it seabed through fog, or black?
  2. If deep ocean shows black, keep depths 7–5 near opaque and make only the shallows translucent.
  3. On OCEAN2 with no mod, run a DFHack probe for a few thousand ticks. Count world.event.ocean_waves entries and OceanWave/SeaFoam flows. This confirms waves spawn in graphics mode and shows how many there are.
  4. Fix the three overlay checks, enable ocean_waves_overlay, and time it the way the FPS series did, with and without the overlay. If DF's own waves are sparse, add the optional shoreline surf.
  5. Restyle mist last, once the waves' look is settled, so the two match.

Limits worth knowing

  • DFHack's renderer hooks (map-render, rendermax) are marked unavailable in this build, so a script can draw on top of DF's map but cannot change how DF draws water.
  • An overlay paints above units. A swimming dwarf under a wave crest is covered by it for the frames it shows.
  • Tokens are stable across recent releases, but a sheet layout can change. The raws mod re-binds by name, so only the PNG would need rework.
  • Nothing in the Steam install, the DFHack prefs or any save was changed during this research.
