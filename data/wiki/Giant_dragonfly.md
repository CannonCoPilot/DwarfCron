# Giant dragonfly

- Giant dragonflies** are much larger versions of the standard vermin dragonfly, appearing in savage lakes.

They aren't very useful for anything other than immediate butchering, as they have a maximum lifespan of one year like other giant insects. And as they are not , wild ones may annoyingly interrupt your dwarves when spotted.

Some dwarves like giant dragonflies for their *faceted eyes*.
