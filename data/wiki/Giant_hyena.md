# Giant hyena

- Giant hyenas** are extremely large, six times the size of an average dwarf, close to as large as a yak. Thanks to the <tt>[LARGE_PREDATOR]</tt> token they inherit from their basal kin, they're also extremely aggressive. Combined with their pack behavior, appearing in groups of 5-15 individuals, giant hyenas are (unsurprisingly) *extremely* dangerous, and will rip apart an unfortunate dwarf faster than he can scream. Should not be approached without large and experienced military hunting parties in full armor; the proceeds of such a clash could feed the fortress for a while.

Some dwarves like giant hyenas for their *distinctive laugh* and their *coordinated hunting*.
