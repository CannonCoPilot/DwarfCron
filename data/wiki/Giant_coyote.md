# Giant coyote

- Giant coyotes** are large dog-like monsters with a haunting howl. They appear in groups of 2-10 in most savage biomes, and are pack hunters that are bigger than elks. Lacking the  token, they aren't very aggressive towards dwarves, though they butcher into a medium-sized amount of food returns, and so may help a struggling fortress to get by.
:*Dwarves like giant coyotes for their howling*.
